* C23 CAS rules
7.17.7.4 paragraphs 2-7: failure ordering excludes release and acq_rel and cannot
exceed success ordering; failure updates expected. Weak CAS can fail when equal.
The loop example rebuilds desired from the latest expected value.
* Release sequence and acquire
5.1.2.4 paragraphs 10-11: an acquire synchronizes with a release when its read
comes from that release sequence. Later RMW operations can extend that sequence.
Numeric equality or a relaxed observation alone does not establish this edge.
The proposed all-acq_rel CAS / acquire-failure policy is a conservative design
choice, not a minimal ordering requirement or a fact about current Gambit macros.
* Storage representation
6.2.6.2 permits padding in general unsigned integer types; 7.22.1.1 exact-width
uintN_t has no padding. CAS equality must match the selected representation.
A minimum-width typedef alone does not prove that requirement.
* Coherence observations
N3096 5.1.2.4 paragraphs 27 and 31 constrain later reads of the same atomic
object by earlier HB reads and writes. Renewing a logical scope does not erase
those edges. Numeric floors apply only under the model's monotone, non-reused
word discipline; they do not reconstruct runtime reads-from identities.
