    The breakdown of the label code is interesting, since it
shows the dynamic execution frequency of the various types
of label. Recall that simple labels and continuations actually
generate no code, so there is no runtime associated with their
use. A procedure label, however, requires an arity check
and may require moving values from argument locations to
parameter locations.
