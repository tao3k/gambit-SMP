## Concrete Research Outcome And Remaining Bindings

1. **Queue disposition:** locate the actual source/caller interval that freezes
   every relevant producer and permits a valid empty snapshot or scanned-root
   transfer. Scheme join observes termination under the thread lock after
   locked owned-queue cleanup; it can precede the target's notification tail
   and current-thread clear. Woken threads have their own lifetime. The existing
   join-before-shrink test sources are not blanket processor-idle witnesses.
2. **Raw origins:** preserve the distinction between thread-slot movement and
   reusable captured continuation origins. Bind save/capture/no-winding graft/
   return, break-frame and GC readers, overflow-undo exclusion and valid maps.
   A heap-retargeted continuation need not keep old raw storage alive forever;
   a still-raw live origin cannot be discharged by a different invocation ending.
3. **Reuse order:** bind the private publisher's active interval and phase/epoch,
   full strong/weak/cleanup barriers, final configured section target and actual
   later allocation. A `max_heap` cap applied after the computed lower bound
   requires compatibility or safe error-path evidence; the algebraic countercase
   is not proof of a reachable native corruption.
