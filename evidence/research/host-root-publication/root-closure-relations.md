## Existing Scheme Relation Contract

The existing `make-parallel-gc-review` constructor produces three scoped clues
with source-lock identity, factors and expandable source/model references:

- `root-scan-edge-source`: pointer storage/PERM identity differs from explicit
  payload scan coverage after processor population shrink.
- `chain-origin-source`: a copied head differs from the last raw origin into
  the older frame chain.
- `phase-consumer-source`: raw-origin closure differs from target-work and
  collector-cycle completion before a later half reuse.

Each is still `source-composition-unbound` despite its checked finite interface.
There is no `composition_link`, `composition_binding` or `satisfies` row. All
model scopes remain `partial`. Withdrawing each of the 12 declared source
identities exposes missing evidence but preserves all three owed requirements.
Removing `interface_check` exposes `model-check-unbound-or-stale`; restoring
inputs restores the baseline result. Before/after source hashes are equal.
No new CLI, relation schema, production Python or workflow logic was introduced.
This was direct existing Scheme evaluation, not a Grok field trial or a claim
that repository-wide discovery registration/native custody was requalified.

## Remaining Source Composition

1. **Physical root graph:** show the actual supported shrink caller's surviving
   roots through runnable, timed, device and interrupt queues and escaped
   continuations. An incoming pointer to a retired PERM processor is not an
   automatic traversal of its movable payload. Show transfer or a valid scoped
   absence/termination witness before deque removal and slot reset.
2. **Old stack region:** show all raw origins are resolved under valid frame
   maps before that same half/incarnation may be overwritten. The arena being
   allocated, head being copied or address/half parity recurring does not prove
   this. Bind default/custom allocation and section-sharing bounds as well.
3. **Last consumers:** show old metadata-read tails, aliases, private target
   scan work and phase barriers separately; then join/remote/host tails for the
   same source/configuration. Root forwarding is not a complete cycle witness.

Chez's explicit TC/continuation traversals and preserved creator carrier, plus
OCaml's stack-parent/handler traversal and marking/consumption handshake,
support these obligations but do not prove them in Gambit. The Recipe preserves
Gambit's C backend, multi-shot continuations and FFI boundaries. Current-repair
models stay in `models/gambit`; general attachment, independent root registries
and changed root/stack ABI remain Experimental, with no redesign selected here.
This research does not change the other thread's reported 9/12 stage count.
