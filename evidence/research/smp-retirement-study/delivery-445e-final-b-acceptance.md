### 5. Drain is predicate-driven service, not a lucky resample

After seal, fresh enters cannot replenish the count. Already accepted native invocations release their own actual units; any authorized completion borrow is attributable to that finite accepted work. P0 retains the ticket and its valid native owner while driving the existing native sync/service loop at a root-safe boundary. It must participate in pending GC/sync needed by the old cohort rather than busy-spin while a worker waits for P0. Never wait while holding T/P/VM/result locks, a journal mutex or an unsaved Scheme-dependent native leaf. A missing legal Scheme context is reported, not bypassed with a global dynamic-wind fallback.

A step may return pending when work needs another existing participant; progress remains in the same ticket. The driver waits/services that explicit dependency with the original total bound. Optional notifications are hints, not proof; the sealed admission/root/join predicates determine the next stage. A new condition wait is not accepted until its lost-wake predicate and SYNC participation are shown. No post-last-leave VM dereference merely to signal a waiter without an independent checked lifetime. Permanent fault, unsupported context, stuck accepted work or the original bound expiring retains precise ownership and fails safely. The distinction from old retry is structural: fresh registration is excluded and stages do not restart; sampling0 alone is never the commit point.
