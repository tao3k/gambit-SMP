Modernization is a joint redesign of semantic generation, execution admission,
root custody, collection, control restoration, foreign effects and native artifact
identity. Modern C is the required realization language. Zig is not required for
any correctness argument below and cannot be credited with benefits obtained by
the same responsibility replacement in C.

The positive architectural claim is specific: replace implicit shared lifetimes
and independently interpreted control state with explicit owners, generated
semantic contracts and composable handoffs. For each covered fault family,
the successor must exclude the bad history under stated premises. A successful
guard that drops work, retained state that grows without bound, or permanently
disabled SMP does not satisfy that claim.

The [[file:../../../docs/smp-twelve-gate-research-map.md][twelve-gate ledger]] is twelve acceptance obligations, not twelve bugs or
twelve processor populations. Its historical 8/12 status belongs to its declared
producer. The eight [[file:../pressure-map.org][research pressures]] are a different classification.
The gate associations below are many-to-many relevance edges, not causal
attribution of every old failure. In particular, the recorded duplicate ordinary
scan is evidence of a reachable conflict; it does not explain every SIGBUS,
Gerbil type failure, numeric defect or integration timeout.

The canonical module layout remains [[file:../../architecture/runtime/models/sysml/composition.sysml][GambitRuntimeComposition]] and migration
ownership remains [[file:../models/sysml/adoption.sysml][GambitRefactoring]]. The [[file:models/sysml/causal-architecture.sysml][causal SysML refinement]] references
those pressure owners; it is the maintained source of the matrix and dependency
diagram. [[file:../../modeling/ascent/causal-architecture.rules.scm][ASCENT]] checks evidence kind, source, artifact, scope and prerequisite
gaps. It must not turn a populated architecture field into a C theorem.

* Conditional exclusion, rather than patch accumulation
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: modern-c-conditional-exclusion
:END:

Let a concrete history include generated entry, C stores, GC barriers, queue
publication, native completion and Scheme resumption. Let alpha map its steps
to the contracts' operations. The architecture excludes a fault family only if:

1. Every legal producer, writer, consumer and exceptional exit is in alpha's
   domain. An untracked raw FFI write is a failed premise, not an invisible case.
2. Initial states satisfy the invariants, and each concrete step refines an
   allowed abstract step, including failed and unknown outcomes.
3. The invariant contradicts the bad history: no second ordinary repair writer,
   no unrooted outstanding request, no reused live incarnation, no flip without
   actual capacity, or no completion with future producers still authorized.
4. Observable Scheme behavior is preserved, including multi-shot control,
   dynamic-wind, exceptions, proper tail calls and valid native-return origins.
5. Progress has separate premises: legal safe points are eventually reached,
   runnable work is serviced, and external operations meet their declared
   completion/failure contract. Safety alone supplies no bounded latency.

Items 1--3 are the theoretical reason the redesign can remove a *class* of
failures. Merely adding an owner name, a boolean seal or a queue without those
transition obligations does not establish them. Items 4--5 prevent obtaining a
safe but semantically weaker or permanently stalled language.

** Custody conservation

For one accounting unit and epoch, accepted work is partitioned into private,
queued, running, transfer-pending and completed/disposed work. These categories
must be disjoint and exhaustive. A parent may finish only after each child has
either a target acceptance or retained source custody. Unknown transfer results
stay transfer-pending; replay queries the same identity instead of accepting a
second task. Counts for roots, bytes and tasks are separate ledgers, not numbers
that may be added together.

This removes the assumption that an empty public queue means no future work.
The fixed point requires all producers closed and all private/descendant debt
discharged. Tail work and frame-link repair are real producers. A finalizer
that schedules a callback is also a producer, even if ordinary marking is done.

** Continuous roots and stable identity

For every outstanding Scheme reference, at least one registered, scannable
slot remains the authoritative custodian. A handoff temporarily permits both
source and destination coverage, but never neither. Destination registration
and scan synchronization precede source ACK; ACK also requires transport drain.
Movement changes a slot's value, so every C consumer reloads after a GC-capable
boundary. The root-cut identity concerns slots, membership incarnation and source
layout, not immutable object addresses.

The current resize replacement is a concrete example. Transport now carries
origin/serial, not a copied movable thunk. Worker zero scans all fixed request
cells, including retained origins outside active membership. A selected request
reloads its registered value; consumed plus drained authorizes withdrawal.
These responsibilities replace the stale-pointer mechanism. An extra root check
at the eventual crash site would leave that mechanism intact.

** Unique work and physical capacity are a joint obligation

A duplicate traversal request may be delivered, but it cannot acquire a second
ordinary field-repair authority. Initialization, ordinary scanning, first-field
Tail and structural frame links have distinct duties. Forwarding visibility is
not payload readiness; raw payload readiness is not semantic frame readiness.
This is a change in the work algorithm, not permission to discard an uncompleted
owner's task. A dead or unknown canonical owner needs explicit takeover or
retention; marking it DONE is invalid.

The allocated-source census U bounds unique copy work only with a complete
source/frame expansion argument. The planner must also know the maximum single
request A, real intervals, chunk links, terminal fragments, stack restoration and
retained domains. Under the existing selected-profile packing premises,
G = 65536 - 16395 - 4 - ceil(65536/255) = 48879 words and
H <= W + floor(U/G). These are conditional packing terms, not an implementation
of a reservation planner or an empirical heap multiplier. Required physical
capacity includes retained Q, stack S, copy H and restoration R without double
counting shared intervals. Known failed private CAS attempts refund unpublished
grants; they cannot refund already committed section waste.

In current C, =___garbage_collect_pstate= flushes fp/hp and marks roots frozen,
then barriers precede =setup_stack_heap_vmstate=. The setup captures a census,
checks the two initial sections per participant and flips/assigns initial
regions. It does *not* reserve the complete injective layout. The next complete
architecture cut must replace that census-to-flip decision with a source-bound
reserve-or-retain transaction, not claim that passing a small workload proves
the missing planner. See [[file:round-capacity.org][the physical layout contract]].

** Progress and unknown native outcomes

Reclamation needs native users to stop; healthy GC need not always wait for
native reclamation. The implemented empty, never-RUN startup domain can detach
heap claims while retaining its unknown-join native record. A published actor,
an unknown creator or a raw borrower is outside that certificate. This is an
explicit authority distinction, not a general promise to recover arbitrary
OS-thread death.

A recoverable failure needs a progress agent that does not wait on the resource
it must release. For a destructor/owner/GC wait cycle, qualified work must be
rooted and accepted for post-resume owner execution; its last consumer must be
accounted for. If an external callback can block forever, the design must expose
a bounded resource/failure policy. Retaining everything forever proves neither
recovery nor a net modernization benefit.

** Full versus delimited; one-shot versus multi-shot
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: continuation-capability-axes
:SOURCE: vendor/gambit/doc/gambit.txi:5186-5221
:END:

These are independent dimensions. Full continuations capture the current
continuation without a programmer-specified prompt boundary; delimited operators
capture a segment up to a delimiter. Either API can have one-shot or multi-shot
resumption semantics. Abortive versus composable invocation and prompt identity
are additional choices, not synonyms for multi-shot.

The actual =frame-continuation-roots.scm= fixture exercises Gambit's full,
multi-shot =continuation-capture=/=continuation-return= interface, with call/cc
escape and dynamic-wind. The same captured continuation returns 20 and 200;
the test checks 80 roots, eleven completed GCs, winding order, native calls and
frame/root decoder agreement. The escape continuation used by the test is not
by itself a primitive prompt/reset implementation. This is not a delimited
continuation qualification.

The architecture should make precise stack segments, roots and resumption
protocols reusable for delimited control and effect handlers. Before admitting
that extension, specify prompt lifetime/identity, segment composition, denv and
wind transitions, multi-shot aliasing, scheduler ownership and legal foreign
boundaries. These questions amplify Scheme's control facilities; they must not
be answered by restricting an existing multi-shot continuation to one use.

* Quantifying the benefit without inventing a speedup

Use a vector of observable work and retained resources rather than one percentage:
ordinary field repairs, copied words, root slots visited, mature graph visits,
barrier/log traffic, rendezvous waits, generated bytes, native actions and peak
live/reserved bytes. A safety improvement and a throughput improvement are
separate outputs.

For an admitted mature graph, the proposed generation-aware path has positive
work benefit only when avoided full-graph visits exceed barrier/logging,
remembered scans, promotion and extra root work at equivalent semantics. For a
build, saved generation/compilation work must exceed validation, planning and
metadata costs. Moving a destructor off a GC pause can reduce pause work while
leaving total release work unchanged. No weighting or workload mix is silently
assumed.

* Architecture questions before a commit
#+NAME: modern-c-causal-questions
| Question | Required answer or refutation |
|---+---|
| Does every legal path use the new owner? | Enumerate generated, native, failure and late-result routes; a bypass prevents retirement |
| Can an unknown owner retain the whole VM forever? | Separate immutable heap-detachment permission from native-close permission; state a bounded policy for unsupported unknowns |
| Can an owner produce work after completion? | Closed producer inventory plus private/child/Tail/release debt; no queue-empty shortcut |
| Does a capacity decision use the same frozen root/source epoch? | Identity-bound source inventory and actual disjoint reserve-or-retain before parity flip |
| Are Scheme control capabilities stronger or merely restricted? | Keep full multi-shot semantics; specify delimited/effects separately; no resurrection of expired C activations |
| What is actually removed? | Old movable sync payload is replaced now; full PSTATE collector ownership, duplicated semantic emission and inline release retire only with all consumers migrated |
| What would falsify the gain? | Uncovered mutation, global serialization, unbounded retention, worse ordinary-path cost or unchanged duplicated owners |
