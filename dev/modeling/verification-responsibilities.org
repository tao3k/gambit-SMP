[[file:models/sysml/verification-responsibilities.sysml][SysML]] is the source of responsibility domains. [[file:ascent/obligation-ownership.rules.scm][ASCENT]] enforces the declared
partition. Each obligation has one canonical semantic key and one owner. Do not
create a Quint and Lean version of the same verification obligation. A component
can depend on different obligations; this is not dual ownership.

#+NAME: verification-responsibility-matrix
#+BEGIN: sysml-view :kind verification-responsibilities :source modeling/models/sysml/verification-responsibilities.sysml
| Owner | Domains | Exclusive responsibility | Excluded responsibility |
|---+---+---+---|
| quint | control | Transition ordering, concurrent interleavings and permit/task/root lifetime protocols within declared bounds | Payload algebra, unbounded data-coherence proofs and claims of concrete native correctness |
| lean | data | General mathematical/data representation properties under explicit hypotheses | A duplicate scheduler, bounded trace tests, wind interleavings and claims that premises automatically hold in C |
| source | source;semantics;refinement | Actual source writers/consumers, semantic correspondence and refinement of imported premises | Declaring an unresolved native/refinement obligation proved by source-token presence or LLM judgment alone |
| native | native | Source/profile-bound execution, semantic regression, progress and measured resource/performance acceptance | Promotion of finite models, conditional laws or historical binaries to new native completion |
#+END:

The LLM is the working environment, not another component, service, verifier
owner or approval gate. Reviewing semantic keys for disguised duplication and
checking source correspondence happens as ordinary work in that environment.
Do not pretend ASCENT can decide arbitrary semantic equivalence between two
natural-language claims. Its checks operate on the reviewed, normalized keys.

* Required workflow

1. Declare distinct questions and semantic keys in the relevant SysML component.
2. Choose one owner for each question from the shared responsibility catalogue.
3. Use ASCENT to reject duplicate IDs/owners, same-key aliases and role/domain
   mismatches. Do not dispatch both tools by default for each component.
4. Run the selected evidence method. Shared interface data is permitted; shared
   proof ownership is forbidden.
5. Keep any cross-model/source correspondence as a separate refinement question
   with its own key. It is not proved merely because both endpoint tools pass.
6. Reuse unchanged evidence by artifact/query hash; preserve failed attempts.

The current [[file:../architecture/runtime/control/continuation-segments.org][continuation study]] applies this policy to eight obligations: one
Quint schedule obligation, one Lean data-coherence obligation and six open
source/native obligations. Actual negative controls reject dual ownership,
renamed same-key duplication, wrong-owner evidence and a wrong responsibility
domain. Six auxiliary Lean lemmas belong to one algebraic obligation, not six
copies of the Quint protocol.

Historical studies and their receipts remain preserved. Existing paired model
links are not silently declared compliant or deleted. Future admission must
identify their distinct questions and remove any duplicated active proof
responsibility before claiming compliance with this policy.
