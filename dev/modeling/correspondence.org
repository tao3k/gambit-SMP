*** Projection and limits
p.handed is one bounded logical root token. p.frozen and p.parked concern final-value handoff, distinct from earlier h record publication. For relocation, abstract original and relocated addresses through stable logical identity; literal p.observed == p.handed is not the Lean address equation.

*** Preconditions
Complete live-slot inventory; host stops relevant writes before handoff; scan sees final writes; relocation mapping and identity abstraction are sound. QNT atomic actions and abstract release/acquire do not prove C weak-memory behavior.

*** Conditional conclusion
Given frozen(slot)=source(slot) and observed(slot)=relocate(handed(slot)), Lean composes observed=relocate(source) for each live slot. The QNT relation covers the selected handoff slice.

*** Counterexample / invalid shortcut
A record can be published while the host still performs its last root store; a reader admitted early can miss it. Equal values by accident do not establish acknowledgement.

*** OPEN Unclosed obligations
No mechanized QNT-to-Lean projection, complete host writer census or C memory-order refinement. Temporal completion additionally assumes model progress and weak fairness.
