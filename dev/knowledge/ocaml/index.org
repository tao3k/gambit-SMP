** Primary mechanism sources
- [[https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.11663][Retrofitting Parallelism onto OCaml]]: collector and barrier choices interact with compatibility, serial overhead and multicore coordination.
- [[https://kcsrk.info/papers/retro-concurrency_pldi_21.pdf][Retrofitting Effect Handlers onto OCaml]]: suspended control has representation and resource consequences, including native boundaries.
- [[https://ocaml.org/manual/5.3/effects.html][OCaml effect-handler semantics]] documents delimited resumptions and their constraints.

** Reflection for Gambit
Learn how execution ownership, roots, barriers and suspended-resource custody form a coherent runtime contract. Preserve Gambit general continuation semantics; do not substitute a one-shot resumption discipline without an explicit restricted API and proof. Choose collector/barrier policy from Gambit's own writes, foreign users and workload assumptions.

** Unresolved
No OCaml benchmark becomes a Gambit forecast. Our continuation/FFI semantics and C profile require independent refinement. [[file:../../modeling/correspondence.org][Our conditional model relations]]
