** Reflection for Gambit
Skipping old graphs is valuable only with complete old-to-young edge coverage, roots and foreign mutation policy. Generation labels alone are insufficient. Foreign deactivation, root retention and reactivation must be composed with Gambit's RC roots, marker allocation and callbacks.
