| RF-P01 | [[https://cs.brown.edu/people/mph/HerlihyW90/p463-herlihy.pdf][Herlihy and Wing, Linearizability (1990), abstract and sections 1--2]] | Separating a pending operation from a legally committed one motivates Permit versus Receipt and the authoritative publication cut. | RF-P03 | [[https://www.llvm.org/docs/GarbageCollection.html#goals-and-non-goals][LLVM Garbage Collection, Goals and non-goals]] | LLVM provides map/poll/barrier support; runtime collector, root/map registration and other lifecycle services remain language-runtime responsibilities. | RF-P04 | [[https://kcsrk.info/papers/retro-parallel_icfp_20.pdf][Sivaramakrishnan et al., Retrofitting Parallelism onto OCaml, ICFP 2020, section 4.4]] | Separate domain synchronization, parallel forwarding and work sharing are explicit. This supports distinguishing quiescence from relocation-writer work; neither their collector algorithm nor measured results are transferred to Gambit. The paper is evidence identity, not a claim about every current OCaml implementation. |
| RF-P05 | [[https://ziglang.org/documentation/0.15.1/#Lifetime-and-Ownership][Zig reference 0.15.1, Lifetime and Ownership]] and [[https://ziglang.org/documentation/0.15.1/#errdefer][errdefer]] | Ownership remains programmer responsibility; private ordinary-error cleanup may be shorter. The LLVM sources were read online for this study; their current wording is not
an installed LLVM/provider ABI qualification. Reference release numbers identify
the inspected documents, not architectural generations. The protocol uses the
selected mechanisms as constraints, with project deductions explicitly marked.

*** Evidence Class and Remaining Gap
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: 8b7efa28-41ac-5e64-9b4f-49a515b9999e
:CUSTOM_ID: evidence-class-and-remaining-gap
:END:

RTP/RTA/RTO/RTF/RFS describe a proposed runtime protocol. RTPR arguments are
handwritten conditional deductions; RTX rows are logical interleavings, not
model-checker/native witnesses. In particular, Prepared manifest != registered
future root, root receipt != epoch completion, and epoch completion != old
facility retirement. The cost matrix evaluates equally capable C/Zig designs;
no language choice discharges the caller, root, queue or all-exit premises.
