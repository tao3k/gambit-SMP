* Benefit and peak-memory matrix
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: interval-benefit-matrix
:END:
#+NAME: interval-benefits-native
| Capability | Removed work | Bound | Added cost | Positive condition and falsifier |
|---+---+---+---+---|
| placement | Eliminate retry after destructive forwarding due only to optimistic capacity counters | Validate every object/segment placement before forwarding; sum capacity alone is insufficient | Preflight extents/layout traversal plus interval/claim/partial-result records | Correctness benefit; no measured reduction of total allocation or pause |
| tail reuse | Avoid fresh context reserve only when transferable suffix already covers an actual demand | 0<=q_i<=min(usableTail_i,requestedResume_i), with alignment/contiguity; Q=sum(q_i) | Transferred tail still belongs to committed destination; protect fudge and prefix indexes | Savings are relative to a naive separated-worker/context design; current same-PSTATE restore already reuses tails |
| peak | Subtract accepted reuse from additional fresh context storage, never from destination and resume twice | Peak=max(phase peaks); pre-copy subtract only preflight-guaranteed Qg, post-copy may also subtract opportunistic Qo; never count either twice | Allocator headers, alignment, claim/layout/task/pin records and two card banks remain charged | Post-copy refunds cannot lower an earlier peak; preflight Qg must exceed any extra destination/metadata/held-partial cost; RSS/OOM still needs platform evidence |
| reader lifetime | Replace implicit phase/pstate lifetime assumptions with explicit last-use custody | One short lifecycle-lock acquire/release per finite borrow; task handoff preserves one token; nested calls counted | Pin records and lock/condvar contention; per-thread generation alone is insufficient | No unparented old reader after retire; derived child acquired before releasing parent; no forced timeout unpin |
| bounded retention | Prevent silent accumulation of old banks and unresolved allocations | Two preallocated card banks; no third epoch-bank publication until the older bank drains | A stalled reader can block progress; finite held-partial budget and unresolved operation slots remain owned | Safety by backpressure/refusal/quarantine, not a liveness or crashed-thread recovery theorem |
