THREAD_SAVE stores a frame into the current thread continuation; THREAD_RESTORE installs the resumed break link and clears that resumed slot. Captured continuation traversal is separate: mem.c scans its raw frame before the remaining fields. Clearing the resumed slot does not consume all previously captured origins.

** State, transitions and necessary invariant
Current break links and separately captured origins -> FrameSpec slot/root coordinates and backing leases.

save/restore/current-root copy/queue saved continuation/traverse; graft is not destructive consumption.

Required: Current-root coverage cannot discard untraversed saved origins; no backing reuse before complete last-user closure.

Abstraction boundary: Selected origin IDs do not model every frame, denv mutation, serialization, dynamic-wind, FFI writer or generated descriptor.

** Elimination and added work

independent; none proves an additional net gain from maintaining Zig.
