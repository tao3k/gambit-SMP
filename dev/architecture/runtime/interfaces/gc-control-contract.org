** Identity and coverage are shared premises
A scan or completion witness identifies VM plus VM generation, collection epoch and root-cut epoch. Root carriers and reclaim targets additionally identify resource generation; a worker-team witness identifies that team's generation. These are logical comparison scopes, not a selected C/Zig layout or newly implemented ABI.

A bounded QNT execution can map its one selected VM/epoch to a fixed identity only within that slice. It cannot thereby prove generation rollover, ABA rejection, cross-worker resumption or merged-request completion. Root-cut/collection epoch correspondence and team identity must be related explicitly; matching one integer alone is insufficient.

For relocation, distinguish a logical root value from its physical address. A valid abstraction preserves logical identity under relocation and preserves distinctions needed to detect stale or wrong values. Mapping every pointer to one token would hide the bug. The host-root model's valid payload 0 is not an absent-root sentinel; its -1 initial observation must not become a valid acquired value.

** Ownership and phase order
1. Control constructs and preserves the origin inventory; execution/FFI custodians publish final values and stop relevant mutation for the admitted root cut. This publication cannot wait for the GC that needs it.
2. GC admits scanners only after the declared root contributors acknowledge and their backing remains live. Current-stack scanning and saved-continuation traversal are different work.
3. Strong traversal resolves or forwards every required raw origin. Copying a continuation container is not completion of its saved-frame traversal.
4. Completion requires private, queued and in-flight scanning work to drain, together with exclusion or separately accounted reopening by producers. Weak processing and cleanup still have their own phase obligations.
5. Backing reuse additionally needs target-specific last-use closure: saved origins, scanners, native returns and deferred foreign cleanup. A Scheme join or an ended foreign frame is not a universal producer-drain witness.
6. Resumption consumes the matching completion identity and reloads moved values under a valid execution grant. General continuation reuse does not consume the original saved origin merely because one invocation returned.

The typed SysML dependency graph covers resolution/completion phases. It does not say that inventory construction depends on GC completion, and it is not a lock/wait graph. Prove publication-before-wait and liveness separately; an acyclic documentation graph cannot certify native deadlock freedom.

** Source slice that is established
[[file:../../../../vendor/gambit/include/gambit.h.in::6314][___THREAD_SAVE]] stores a frame into the current thread's continuation. [[file:../../../../vendor/gambit/include/gambit.h.in::6377][___THREAD_RESTORE]] installs the resumed frame at the break link and clears that resumed continuation slot. These literal source writes establish the selected save/restore distinction, not the lifetime of every captured continuation.

[[file:../../../../vendor/gambit/lib/mem.c::3898][mark_continuation]] scans current frames and follows BREAK_FRAME_NEXT. The [[file:../../../../models/gambit/continuation_saved_stack_origin.qnt][saved-origin slice]] distinguishes that active path from another thread's retained origin. The current-source characterization is reusable; whole-origin completeness and source-to-model simulation remain open.

[[file:../../../../vendor/gambit/lib/mem.c::6938][___garbage_collect_pstate]] orders setup, strong marking, weak marking, cleanup and resize with barriers. This is source-order evidence. The enabled barrier implementation, ownership transitions, independent worker redesign and all callers still need refinement.

** Writer families still requiring census closure
[[file:../../../../vendor/gambit/lib/_kernel.scm::3154][continuation-frame-set!]], [[file:../../../../vendor/gambit/lib/_kernel.scm::3161][continuation-denv-set!]] and [[file:../../../../vendor/gambit/lib/_kernel.scm::3439][frame-set!]] are real mutation entry points in addition to thread save/restore. A complete control/root contract must trace their actual callers, generated writes, reconstruction and exceptional restoration paths. Naming these entries does not establish that the writer census is complete or that all mutations participate in a proposed generational barrier. RH-CENSUS, CO-CENSUS and CO-RESUME remain open.

** Conditional mathematical support and its limits
[[file:../../../../models/gambit/lean/HostRootParticipation.lean][frozenHandoffComposesRelocation]] composes handed=source with observed=relocate(handed) for every live slot. It does not prove the inventory, handoff or scanner premises.

[[file:../../../../models/gambit/lean/SavedStackRetirementWindow.lean][The saved-stack companion]] supplies no-arrival/empty-set, preserved-origin, complete-inventory and phase-transitivity statements. Historical reports are retained separately; these conditions do not arise from a source variable's name or a separately successful component model.

[[file:../../../../models/gambit/lean/WorkAccounting.lean][zeroCountAndEmptyQueuesExcludeHiddenWork]] requires every private producer to remain counted and every declared queue to be empty. Worker has counted/privateWork/queued fields; it has no separate scanning field. To use it here, establish actualScanning(w) implies counted(w), complete worker enumeration and no counter overflow. Then zeroCountExcludesActive rules out scanning. Without those additional premises, the theorem cannot establish complete scanner drain.
