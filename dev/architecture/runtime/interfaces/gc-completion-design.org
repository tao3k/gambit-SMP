** What Gambit's actual producers do
The [[file:completion-model-map.org][SysML-derived source ledger]] records 12 anchor ranges and limits. The important chain is concrete:

1. setup_stack_heap_vmstate initializes distinct strong and weak scan-worker counts to processor_count. Root marking begins while participants are counted, including work before entering the local scan loop.
2. mark_array copies with a local allocation cursor. A forwarding CAS may become visible before all payload words are copied. end_heap_chunk later publishes initialized chunk content with ordered barriers. A forwarding address is not a scanner-ready work receipt.
3. mark_captured_continuation copies raw saved frames and may end/publish another chunk. Traversing a saved continuation can create more collector work even when a public FIFO was momentarily empty.
4. The local fixed point alternates still-object and movable-object scanning. Movable work includes the incomplete local chunk, complete FIFO chunks and the chunk currently being scanned. Still scanning can generate movable or still work.
5. A counted worker steals a complete chunk and returns to the local fixed point. It decrements its phase count under scan_termination_mutex only after its local/steal path; a wake increments the count when termination has not occurred. Publication uses a chunk lock and barriers, not that termination mutex. Source-to-memory and producer-lifetime proof must connect the two.
6. Strong drain is followed by a collective barrier and process_wills in the weak phase. process_wills calls mark_array on wills and selected testators. This is legitimate later work in the same collection. Weak scan completion is followed by move_continuation.
7. Cleanup processes GC hash tables and frees unreachable still wrappers. free_unmarked_still_objs invokes release_foreign, then frees the aligned wrapper. release_foreign clears its pointer/release function before calling native code and may return an error; this caller does not inspect that error. Native-resource success cannot be inferred from wrapper reclamation.
8. Another barrier precedes processor-zero resize_heap. The overall function later prepares execution state and returns overflow. A phase counter reaching zero is earlier than those operations. Capacity-request success and completion/custody are distinct outcomes.

These are source facts for the inspected branches. The single-threaded configuration replaces BARRIER with an empty macro; no SMP memory-order result is assigned to every profile. Worker census, supported native callback behavior and the real enabled profile remain qualification premises.

** Producer closure permits accounted derived work
Closing external enrollment means new unrepresented roots, callbacks or workers cannot enter the phase. An already admitted producer may still discover a child while retaining a valid producer right. Therefore external seal alone does not authorize a drain receipt.

Let P be admitted producer rights and let I, Q, S, T and C be private, queued, scanning, transfer and reserved-child work obligations. A phase-drain cut requires a complete producer census, external enrollment closed, P empty and every work class empty under the same admission/closure order. These are logical sets or counts, with a disjointness/completeness premise. They do not prescribe a new per-object atomic counter; Gambit's worker/chunk accounting may be used after a proved projection.

The parent reserves and commits the child's custody before dropping the parent's final right. Moving work through private -> queue -> scanner changes its owner, not its existence. A transfer failure leaves the source custodian live until another owner has acknowledged custody. Required reachable-object scanning cannot be canceled merely to make the count zero. Partial publication and reserved-but-not-visible children remain obligations at the probe.

** Late-work classification
#+NAME: late-work-custody
| Arrival window | Admission / action | Custody condition |
|---+---+---|
| Before external seal | Enroll into the current phase | Matching scope and live root/backing |
| After seal, admitted parent still active | Accept a derived child | Reserve/register child before parent's final release; phase not closed |
| Races with phase close | Serialize against the close cut | Winner is accounted; losing old-phase admission cannot enqueue |
| After strong drain, weak seed admitted | Accept weak work in its own scope | Matching collection; new weak-phase producer census and barriers |
| Before completion, publication/transfer fails | Retain for retry or declared failure | A valid prior lease/root and source owner still exist |
| After whole-collection completion | Admit a genuinely new request only through a new collection protocol | Stable relocated handle/root; no relabeling of an unresolved raw origin |
| After target permit or physical release | Reject new old-target borrowing | No dereference of released backing; an unregistered raw pointer cannot acquire a lease retroactively |

Late-work rejection is not a recovery proof. A valid lease that pins the exact target would have prevented its permit from being issued. Once backing has actually been released, neither a new epoch number nor an exception handler restores it. The no-unknown-user premise must be proved before granting permission.

** Target cuts preserve existing release timing
#+NAME: target-reclaim-cuts
| Target | Required cut in the proposed contract | Source relationship / unresolved evidence |
|---+---+---|
| Unreachable still wrapper | Relevant tracing/weak cut, native release call returned, wrapper users closed | Current cleanup frees it before full collection return. Any deferred root/payload must have durable successor custody first. |
| Movable/stack backing considered for shrink or reuse | Required raw origins/relocation and continuation move complete, post-cleanup phase cut, no remaining target users | resize_heap follows cleanup barrier; complete physical bounds, callers and native-origin closure remain open. |
| Native payload/resource | Its release contract, owner-thread and asynchronous result/last-use cut | Clearing the Scheme wrapper's pointer or ignoring a return code does not prove native disposal. |
| Execution/context storage or code metadata | Relevant collection delivery plus its own saved/foreign return-user closure | A collection receipt alone does not enumerate these users. |

CollectionCompletion may coexist with deferred native cleanup if durable custody and supported future entry are established. It must not wait for every unrelated I/O action. ReclaimPermission for a target still in use remains denied. This separates collection progress from resource shutdown and avoids requiring a foreign task to run while a collector waits for it under ownership the task needs.

The permit binds resource identity, resource generation, the required cut and the closed admission generation. Consumption is serialized and single-use. The SysML consumption predicate requires a known issued permit, matching actual target, a previously unused permit and a consumed post-state. A proof still has to establish issuer uniqueness, atomic consumption and no late borrower; booleans in a definition do not issue authority.

** Conditional no-reopen argument
Assume complete producer enumeration, no external enrollment after seal, every derived child charged before its parent exits, complete/disjoint private/queue/scan/transfer accounting, correct publication visibility, and one serialization order for admission and closure.

If a later same-phase work item exists after a zero-work/zero-right cut, it must originate from an external entrant, an admitted producer, or an in-flight transfer. The first contradicts the seal/census; the second contradicts no producer rights and parent-child accounting; the third contradicts complete zero transit/reservation accounting. Thus no later same-phase work is admitted under those premises. A weak-phase seed is outside that phase identity, while all global root and backing premises remain required.

This is a conditional mathematical argument. It is not a new Lean proof, a proof of source census, or a C weak-memory theorem.

** QNT correction design
The direct rescan guard already exists and is preserved. The remaining model work is a structured refinement:

- Treat CollectionContract.done as the selected item/phase model's terminal fact. Its current step has no explicit terminal stutter. A future totalized terminal action must keep scope and state unchanged; it is not permission to hide an enabled producer.
- Represent external sealing separately from admitted producer rights and work in transit. Close may succeed only after every required source is represented and all obligations drain. A guard added to one action does not construct this census.
- Preserve valid pre-close rescan/derived work and negative controls for omitted producer lifetime, early publication, stale identity and premature close. Never obtain safety by globally disabling useful pre-close work.
- Reuse [[file:../../../../models/gambit/gc_work_closure.qnt][gc_work_closure]] for its counted workers, graph-discovered child and two phase transition. Its nextPhase changes phase and reopens phase work without inventing a new collection epoch. The supporting slice is finite and atomically abstracts queues and counts; it is not a complete native refinement.
- Model target permission after its required cut, with racing borrower, failed transfer, delayed foreign delivery and stale/duplicate consumption controls. A terminal scan state is insufficient as the permission predicate.

No existing .qnt file is edited, interpreted, simulated or checked here. The correction is a selected design and an obligation inventory.
