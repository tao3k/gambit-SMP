unique formal sources, with their original scopes. DEV owns the target roles,

** The selected source chain already includes parallel collection
Gambit has processor-cooperative collection, chunk stealing and phase termination.
The modernization problem is their ownership and lifecycle coupling. It would be
incorrect to describe current Gambit as having no parallel collector or to count
its existing mechanisms as newly created benefits.

* RESEARCH Ownership and mathematical domains
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: t0-role-ownership
:END:

#+NAME: t0-role-ownership-matrix
| Role | Owns / guarantees | Does not authorize | Important identity |
|---+---+---+---|
| Execution context and grant | Coherent GVM/control state and exclusive managed entry; park/reentry and relocated-root reload | Scanning because its thread blocked; reclaim because TLS was cleared | VM incarnation, context incarnation, owner-thread incarnation, grant serial |
| Epoch participant | A declared contribution to a sealed collection census; stable locations and final-value handoff or a separately admitted inactive contribution | Scheme execution; arbitrary heap scanning; removing another contribution | VM incarnation, participant incarnation, membership generation, request/root epoch, carrier generation |
| Collector work context | Phase/team-scoped task, copy initialization, private cursor, Tail and dequeued work custody | Running Scheme, migrating a native callback or invoking arbitrary foreign cleanup | VM incarnation, request/phase/round, team identity/generation, worker incarnation and accepted task |
| Coordinator | Membership cut, producer closure, accepted request set, common completion/capacity and resource-specific drain | Treating worker count as the participant census | Request/result identity and the exact cohort it completes |

Let E be context incarnations, P(e,g) the participant set admitted for request e
and membership generation g, and W(e,p,r) the worker team for phase p and round r.
There is no requirement that their cardinalities are equal. A VM/global root
contribution is not a suspended Scheme context; a parked foreign root contributor
need not be an available worker. One OS thread may execute different roles in
sequence, but each transition must hold the appropriate authorization.

~owner : E ⇀ Grant~ must be a partial function. ExclusiveContextGrantLaw is a
pairwise necessary condition: duplicated references to the same grant are allowed;
distinct live owners of one context incarnation are not. A global theorem also
needs the complete grant inventory and serialized acquire/revoke transitions.
A pair of arbitrary records does not establish that theorem.

For every required live root location there must be a supported contribution and
an admitted scanner update/reload path. This is coverage, not a claim that every
contribution has disjoint slots. Aliases, current/saved/heap continuation origins,
denv, RC payloads and external roots retain their own writer and lifetime duties.
Incarnations are logical identities, not unbounded native counters: integer width,
wrap/ABA exclusion and storage reuse still need a concrete C refinement.

WorkContextDomainLaw also matches the worker's scope against the coordinator's
active root cut, phase, round, producer generation and team. Matching a collection
epoch alone cannot admit delayed strong-phase work during a later weak round.
The coordinator reference must be an authoritative coherent snapshot, not another
worker-local cached record; its publication and grant revocation remain C/QNT
refinement duties. CollectorWorkContext currently scopes strong/weak scan and
copy work; legal cleanup delivery remains a separate phase/service obligation.

* RESEARCH Shared protocol, custody and error delivery
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: t0-role-protocol
:END:

#+NAME: t0-role-protocol-cuts
| Cut | Required before crossing | Responsibility after crossing / failure |
|---+---+---|
| Admit request and member | Stable VM/member incarnation; enrollment serialized with the membership cut; accepted request inputs known | Coordinator retains unknown or losing request dispositions; no completion inferred from a higher-priority operation |
| Freeze context contribution | Final root stores and complete writer/location census; exclude further managed aliases/mutation | Execution rights cease before scan; participant and carrier keep final values and metadata alive |
| Publish acknowledgement | Match request, membership, participant incarnation, carrier generation and acknowledged final-value version | A published owner pointer or equal value by accident is insufficient; release/acquire or collective ordering must be established |
| Grant scan | Sealed membership, matching active VM/phase/team, task/descriptor leases, established scanner acquire | Scanner owns accepted work; no dependence on worker ID equalling the originating processor |
| Transfer work / withdraw | Accept complete copy/frontier/Tail/dequeued-chunk state and phase debt before releasing sender custody | Rejected or unknown transfer retains sender responsibility; a forwarding identity does not settle initialization debt |
| Complete collection | All required root contributions, producers and private/queued/claimed/descendant/weak/cleanup duties closed | Publish one coherent result and capacity/accounting view for the accepted request cohort |
| Resume managed context | Coherent committed relocation, scanner/writer drain, accepted result, live exclusive grant, reload and pending-request reconciliation | Capacity failure may be delivered as a Scheme error after coherent GC; it is not permission to resume through unknown layout state |
| Remove participant / release storage | Close ingress; settle required epoch or commit replacement membership/root custodian; drain readers | Participant removal, work-grant release, OS-thread join and backing reclamation remain separate events |

RoleResumeLaw deliberately does not require ~capacitySatisfied~. The source GC
can complete relocation yet report insufficient requested capacity. Conflating
capacity success with coherent root state would suppress legal error delivery.
Rollback after partial relocation, cancelled collection and unknown completion
remain separate open protocols; no resume is granted merely to force progress.

The eight new necessary laws must be used together with the existing root,
control, phase-closure and target-reclaim laws. Defining a requirement neither
asserts it satisfied nor makes its booleans trustworthy. In particular, native
locks/atomics must establish both descriptor publication and final-value handoff.
Existing W_ALL/R_ALL, queue barriers and synchronization must be reused or replaced
under a correspondence proof, not charged again as automatically new fences.

The participant model admits VM and foreign contributions without inventing a
dummy executing context. A blocked native call is eligible only after its actual
roots and aliases are governed. Nested callbacks reacquire execution separately;
they cannot reuse a parked parent's authorization. Native stack, dynamic-wind,
continuation and destructor-thread restrictions are not relaxed by worker migration.

** Source-specific open root and cleanup duties
RC registration uses the selected PSTATE chain at [[file:../../../../vendor/gambit/lib/mem.c::723][mem.c:723–738]], mark_rc traverses
that chain at [[file:../../../../vendor/gambit/lib/mem.c::3969][3969–3990]], and the common-root branch calls it for processor zero.
The target must close actual registration owners and every admitted chain; these
lines alone do not prove a currently reachable nonzero-owner failure. Neither
zero workers nor an empty queue proves that RC/root carriers may be reclaimed.

The marker allocation TODO at [[file:../../../../vendor/gambit/lib/c_intf.c::6511][c_intf.c:6511–6547]] remains a real source obligation:
protect managed input before allocation, retain nested invocation bindings and
deliver exception cleanup under the correct native owner. An independent collector
cannot fix that by taking over an unprotected pointer. Foreign release admission
must reuse the existing release-coordination research and actual callback census.

| Existing binding | Actual reusable scope | Target refinement still required |
|---+---+---|
| ROOT-HANDOFF | Frozen final-value handoff, scanner eligibility and conditional relocation algebra | All real contributors/writers, native acquire/release, nesting, incarnation and complete membership |

The separate gc_work_closure.qnt is supporting bounded work/phase research, not
the primary selected QNT of GC-WORK-CLOSURE. Its two workers are not a proof that
the real worker set equals the participant set. Preserve the withdrawn direct
terminal-reopen trace classification: current guards exclude that trace; the
remaining gap is source producer/phase/permit refinement.

#+NAME: t0-role-new-law-matrix
| New necessary law | Proposed owning QNT obligation | Required Lean relation / missing premise |
|---+---+---|
| ContextGrantLaw | admit/park/reconcile/reenter with distinct context/grant incarnations | Coherent GVM root/control image and native checkpoint correspondence |
| ExclusiveContextGrantLaw | serialized acquire/revoke, duplicate reads, nested callback | Partial-function owner map; complete inventory, no ABA and source lock/atomic refinement |
| WorkContextDomainLaw | accepted task under active VM/phase/round/team | Scope-preserving task projection; queue publication and scan-read readiness |
| WorkCustodyReleaseLaw | accept/reject/unknown migration with copy/frontier/Tail/dequeued debt | Exactly retained sender or committed receiver custody; layout and phase-accounting conservation |
| ParticipantAcknowledgementLaw | register/freeze/final-store/ack/seal under incarnation and membership change | Complete root/writer census, final value observation and native ordering |
| ParticipantRemovalLaw | close/replace/settle/drain with late scan or callback | Removal preserves coverage; actual last-reader and storage lifetime |
| RoleScanHandoffLaw | context participant versus VM/foreign contributor; independent worker selection | All acknowledgement/work laws conjoined; root/frame/metadata and scanner grant correspondence |
| RoleResumeLaw | common completion, capacity error, pending next request and reentry | Reload/alias invalidation and coherent error delivery; no unknown-layout resume |

These are planned states/steps and theorem obligations, not new authored QNT
actions or checked Lean theorems. A future target model must relate initial
states, every real step/stutter and complete failure histories. Progress needs
fairness, enabled work and finite retained-resource assumptions separately.

ASCENT joins the four native-resolved QNT/Lean pairs to the unchanged existing
component association hypotheses under their exact source/bundle identity. A
small JSON/record transport carries native values; it evaluates no model rules.
The target-model and native-output digests are explicit caller-verified bindings,
not tool-attested proof observations. The fine-grained FL adapter remains OPEN.
ASCENT cannot turn a source association, historical theorem or structural pass
into collector correctness, native acceptance or an LLM execution permission.

* RESEARCH Net benefit and incremental cost
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: t0-role-benefit-matrix
:END:

For matched semantic work let T0 be current runtime cost. The theoretical target
delta is new grant/registry/handoff/drain and changed contention cost minus
provably removed coordination, repeated policy work and avoidable waiting.
Positive gain requires that signed delta to be negative while preserving all
supported outcomes, progress and bounded retention. Role separation alone
eliminates zero required live-root visits and zero necessary copied payload words.

#+NAME: t0-role-net-benefit-matrix
| Mechanism | Eligible removal | Required baseline | Added delta / quantification | Positive condition and refutation |
|---+---+---+---+---|
| Explicit execution grant | Repeated implicit owner-selection/reentry policy | Root stores, reload, Scheme/control effects | Per entry/park/reentry grant checks; track transitions and actual synchronization | Generated/inlined adapters retire old policy; wrapping PSTATE without retirement is negative support gain |
| Epoch contribution census | Requiring every blocked contributor to act as a collector solely for root ownership | Visit every required root location and govern aliases | O(number of admitted contributions) metadata and logical acknowledgements; batching may add zero distinct fences | Governed inactive contributors permit progress; unbounded FFI mutation defeats admission |
| Independent work context | Provably avoidable scan imbalance or waits tied to mutator ownership | Existing parallel scan, chunk stealing, copied/scanned bytes | O(worker team + in-flight transfers) storage; grant/transfer/queue/idle events | Saved idle/coordination cost exceeds new events under equal work; extra threads or bandwidth contention can reverse it |
| Common result/coordinator | Repeated request/branch/capacity policy and divergent completion ownership | Current priority and still-word aggregation semantics | Membership/producer closure and one accepted result disposition per request cohort | Reuse collective edges; extra coordinator serialization or polling can lose the benefit |
| Participant/worker/resource separation | Lifetime coupling and unnecessary whole-context retention, when last users close | Supported roots, native returns, metadata last use and joins | Reader leases, unknown-outcome retention and drain operations; bound retained bytes and wait obligations | Required custody actually transfers and old storage roles retire; permanent retention is a refutation |
| Later generational policy | Eligible repeated mature-object traversal after writer/barrier closure | All necessary live roots, young traversal and retained strong/weak semantics | Remembered edges, barrier events, promotion and capacity reserve | Avoided mature traversal exceeds added work; this is a separate collector decision, not a reward automatically caused by role separation |

E contexts, P contributions and W workers describe logical records; no physical
heap allocation, atomic counter per object, or OS thread per record is prescribed.
Modern generated C can supply direct role-specific field access. Zig is not
needed to express these contracts, and adding a second runtime language has no
automatic net benefit. No guessed speedup, processor count or person-day estimate
substitutes for this work/space/synchronization vector.

Study [[https://kcsrk.info/papers/retro-parallel_icfp_20.pdf][Retrofitting Parallelism onto OCaml]], especially §1 and §4: its C API
and serial performance requirements constrain collector selection, and its
parallel minor design introduces safepoints while retaining local allocation.
For Gambit, the lesson is to derive collector and participation choices from its
own C-facing, continuation and scheduling invariants. The paper does not prove
this independent-worker design or transfer its performance results to Gambit.
