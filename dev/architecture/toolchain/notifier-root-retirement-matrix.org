** Gain

| key | avoided | added | condition |
|---+---+---+---|
| dead-root-retention | Eliminate notifier-attributable retention after a certified cut; no forced extra GC assumed | Compile-time root/effect proof plus point-specific retirement work | Retiring K proven-dead root locations costs O(K); no whole-heap walk per notice; K and runtime speed unmeasured |
| repeated-control | One canonical attempt slot prevents replayed notices from repeating action or granting another receipt | O(W) live slots and constant identity checks per delivery; retry/error custody | Root-bearing multi-shot captures stay legal outside restricted profile but cannot qualify this attempt |
