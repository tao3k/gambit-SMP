*** Primary Source Links and Revision Limits
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: b5413651-2d20-55bf-b96a-818b67ffc023
:CUSTOM_ID: primary-source-links-and-revision-limits
:END:

- [[https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/tapi/blob/tapi-1300.0.6.5/tools/libtapi/LinkerInterfaceFile.cpp][TAPI 1300]] and [[https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/tapi/blob/tapi-1600.0.11.8/tools/libtapi/LinkerInterfaceFile.cpp][TAPI 1600]] are named published revisions. Neither is identified as the installed reader. Their tag listings associate abbreviated commits c8c4d8e and b824911 respectively; these are not full content hashes.
- [[https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/ld64/blob/main/src/ld/parsers/textstub_dylib_file.cpp][ld64 stub reader]] is a public reference path; new linkers need their own mapping.
- [[https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/dyld/blob/dyld-1378/dyld/DyldProcessConfig.cpp][dyld-1378 ProcessConfig]] and [[https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/dyld/blob/dyld-1378/dyld/DyldDelegates.cpp][delegates]] were read at named revision. [[https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/dyld/blob/main/mach_o/GradedArchitectures.cpp][GradedArchitectures]], [[https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/dyld/blob/main/mach_o/Architecture.cpp][Architecture]], [[https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/dyld/blob/main/common/MachOFile.cpp][MachOFile]] and [[https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/dyld/blob/main/dyld/Loader.cpp][Loader]] were read from rolling main; a tagged GradedArchitectures URL failed. No content equality with that tag or this host is claimed.
- [[https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/dtrace/blob/dtrace-413/lib/libdtrace/common/dt_open.c][dtrace-413 open]] and [[https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/dtrace/blob/dtrace-413/lib/libdtrace/common/dt_consume.c][consume]] bind named-revision paths. [[https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/dtrace/blob/main/lib/libdtrace/common/dt_handle.c][Handlers]] and [[https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/dtrace/blob/main/lib/libdtrace/common/dt_pid.c][pid provider]] remain rolling references.
- [[https://clang.llvm.org/docs/PointerAuthentication.html][Clang pointer-auth ABI]] is current upstream documentation, not evidence of local Apple Clang or Zig behavior.

This ledger deliberately does not stitch these releases into one proven shipped
stack. Conditional paths in the design require a coherent installed-stack
receipt. A source branch is sufficient to reject a universal incompatibility
claim, but not sufficient to admit the particular host/profile.
