* Defect D-MEM-1: Retirement of Unowned File-Static Section Caches

** Discovery via Source-Bound CBMC and GenMC

Bounded C verification (=c-resize=) and static-state auditing across
=vendor/gambit/lib/_mem/= isolated three unowned mutable caches introduced to
accelerate single-threaded bootstrap compilation:

1. =vendor/gambit/lib/_mem/copy-reservations.inc= (=scan_state_section=,
   =scan_state_base=, =scan_state_offset=, =scan_state_epoch= inside
   =change_object_scan_state=):
   Concurrent collector workers (=W >= 2=) racing on =Section A= and =Section B=
   overwrite =scan_state_section= between the half-range check and the section
   load, claiming =GC_SCAN_OWNED= in the wrong section's scan bitmap and
   aborting at scan completion with =GC scan owner lost before completion=.
   Witnessed mechanically under GenMC =rc11= (=axis1_convergence_contracts.qnt=
   and =WorkerOwnedCacheRefinement.lean=).
2. =vendor/gambit/lib/_mem/movable-sections.inc= (=msection_find_cache[4096]=,
   =msection_find_gen= inside =find_msection=):
   - Unsynchronized struct reads/writes across collector workers;
   - Same-thread stale-index hazard inside =adjust_msections=: bumping
     =msection_find_gen= once before =while (ns < n)= leaves cached =pos=
     entries stale on the *same* thread when inserting two or more sections
     (=statelessOrPerInsertShiftSafeTest= in
     =dev/refactoring/modern-c/models/quint/axis1_convergence_contracts.qnt=);
   - Breaks the =find-section.inc= verbatim projection in =c-resize= and drifts
     =evidence/scenarios/tool-reasoning/c-contracts.view.json=.
3. =vendor/gambit/lib/_mem/copy-planning.inc= (=census_section=, =census_base=,
   =census_offset=, =census_epoch= inside =census_claim=):
   Comparing =epoch= without VM identity collides when two VMs share an epoch
   number on a host thread (=workerScopedCensusCrossVmSafeTest=).

** Unified Axis 1 Resolution

- Retire =msection_find_cache= and =msection_find_bump= from
  =movable-sections.inc=, restoring =find_msection= to a pure read-only binary
  search over =ms->sections= (restoring exact SHA-256 parity with
  =c-contracts.view.json=).
- Move =scan_state_section_= and =census_section_= into
  =struct ___collector_worker_struct= (=vendor/gambit/lib/collector.h=), which
  is already passed as =___collector_worker *work= to =change_object_scan_state=
  and =census_claim= and is intrinsically owned by one collector worker within
  one =work->vm=.
- Proved sound and non-interfering in
  =dev/refactoring/modern-c/models/lean/WorkerOwnedCacheRefinement.lean=.

* Decision C2: Single-Acquire =copy_gate= Fast-Path Admission

- *Observation*: In =vendor/gambit/lib/_mem/allocation-limits.inc=,
  =___group_demand_enter= previously invoked =___group_demand_local_admit=
  followed by =group_demand_local_room=, and =group_demand_local_room= invoked
  =___owned_stack_span= (which acquires =section->copy_gate=) twice --- once for
  containment and a second time inside =mutator_stack_adjust_fits= --- yielding
  up to 4 spinlock acquisitions per write-group when =*hp= advances ahead of
  =ps->context->hp=.
- *Resolution*: Synchronize =ps->context->hp = *hp= and =ps->context->fp = *fp=
  once on fast-path entry when =phase != 2=, verify pointer-local heap bounds
  first, acquire =___owned_stack_span(ps, &lo, &hi)= once, and evaluate
  =stack_span_adjust_fits(lo, hi, fp, stack_words)= on that single =(lo, hi)=
  snapshot.
- *Verification*: Proved equivalent with <= 1 fast-path gate acquisition by
  CBMC (=2120= + =1624= checks) and
  =singleAcquireCopyGateEquivalenceTest= in =axis1_convergence_contracts.qnt=,
  matching =DefaultLeaseComposition.lean=.

* Decision C1: Structured =WriteGroupPlan= and =emitSpec= Coverage Gate

- *Observation*: =vendor/gambit/gsc/_t-c-2.scm= currently represents an open
  write-group via an untyped 8-slot vector and mutates emitted AST strings via
  =set-car!= in =targ-expand-pending-write-group=, without emitting a sidecar
  specification (=emitSpec=) or running =verifyCoverage= as modeled in
  =EmissionView.lean= and =emission_view_correspondence.qnt=.
- *Resolution*: Introduce the explicit =WriteGroupPlan= algebraic lifecycle
  (=openPhase -> committedPhase=) proved in
  =dev/refactoring/modern-c/models/lean/WriteGroupPlanRefinement.lean=, delaying
  dimension formatting until =commitPlan= and emitting a structured
  =write-group-spec= record per group so =verifyCoverage= mechanically checks
  =obsStack <= stackWords= and =obsHeap <= heapWords= before module close.
