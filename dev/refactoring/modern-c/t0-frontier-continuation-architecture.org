* 1. Executive Summary & Why Chez Alone Is Not Enough

First-class continuations are a T0 core capability of Scheme. Auditing =vendor/chez/c/schsig.c=,
=vendor/ocaml/runtime/fiber.c=, and =vendor/gambit/lib/_mem/continuation-roots.inc= alongside
2020--2025 PLDI/OOPSLA/ICFP literature shows that while *Chez Scheme 10* (PLDI '90 / PLDI '96 /
ICFP '20) represents the pinnacle of single-core/traditional-threaded Scheme continuations, it
cannot serve as the sole blueprint for a *12+ core SMP runtime with concurrent SATB GC, Immix,
LXR deferred-RC, and million-fiber concurrency*.

This document synthesizes *6 orthogonal frontier research lines* (12 landmark papers and
production runtimes) into a unified *T0 Frontier Continuation Architecture* for Gambit SMP.

* 3. Architectural Synthesis: Frontier Gambit SMP T0 Continuation Stack

Combining all 6 dimensions yields a 4-layer architecture that surpasses both Chez Scheme and
OCaml 5 individually:
