** Path-level custody

The source model lists exact paths. Tests travel with their owning responsibility; generated artifacts travel with their exact producer. Headers/build support can advance in parallel when independently qualified. Roots → physical reservation → worker/restoration → resource retirement is a dependency chain, not one giant REV. Scheduler and CFFI consumers follow their admitted shared interfaces. CI is published only after the referenced names and recipes are stable.

Every submitted cut requires five gates and a commit body stating tests, benefit, added cost, audit position and remaining boundary. Unvalidated prototypes remain pending; no predicted schedule or plan row is counted as completed.
