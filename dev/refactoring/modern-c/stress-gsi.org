** Correct units before deciding the next runtime design

The [[file:../../../evidence/research/retained/modern-c/stress-gsi/allocation-reassessment.json][allocation reassessment]] preserves the initial raw values and corrects
only their interpretation. =words_movable_objs= in the actual native source
multiplies occupied stack/heap words by2; still-space terms are separate.
The [[file:../../../evidence/research/retained/modern-c/stress-gsi/accounting-geometry.json][configured C23 static assertions]] confirm8-byte words and one vector
header word. =macro-make-rte= and the normal generated =cprc-prc-req1= path
allocate a3-slot vector:32 bump bytes and64 movable occupied counter units.
The source predicts384,064,000 such units for6,001,000 busy-sleep calls in the
1000-delay diagnostic, versus384,171,936 reported. The near match is explanatory
source evidence, not instrumentation of every full-workload allocation.

Neither reported units nor their extrapolation are RSS or OS allocation volume;
do not mechanically halve the entire statistic because stack/still terms differ.
There is no evidence here that every busy-loop iteration captures a continuation.
The identified normal-path work is interpreter environment materialization and
node/application dispatch. A collector or lock change alone does not establish
that the original TFIB fits its fixed budget.

The next complete source responsibility starts at =cprc-prc-req1=,
=macro-make-rte=, local reads/writes and closure creation. Any deferred environment
must retain exact lexical locations, not merely current argument values. Before
closure escape, reflection or multi-shot capture, it needs stable materialized
identity/root custody; resumed code must read/write that same location. Blindly
reusing one mutable RTE across tail calls would corrupt retained captures.
Existing [[file:../../architecture/runtime/control/frame-alias-contract.org][CS-BRIDGE/CS-WRITERS source premises]] govern shared identity,
relocation, writes and restore. Complete caller/escape coverage and real captured
continuation tests are prerequisites to implementation, not duplicate QNT/Lean
proofs. This remains an open design frontier; no frame/GC/continuation change is
claimed by the callable fix, and complete original family execution is still owed.

The [[file:../../../evidence/research/retained/modern-c/stress-gsi/rte-source-frontier.json][canonical three-file frontier]] records140 literal occurrences in the
interpreter, its macros and reflection consumers. It is not a complete dynamic
caller or FFI audit. In particular, procedure closures expose =rte= at slot3;
continuation reflection obtains =$code= and =rte= through their environment map.
The ordinary one-argument entry deliberately keeps both names live before
=check-heap-limit=. Destructive reuse based only on tail position would alias
environments retained by escaped closures or multi-shot continuations. Any
future deferred representation must materialize the current lexical locations
before GC, hooks, callbacks and capture, then preserve those locations through
reflection and repeated resume. No benchmark-specific loop replacement is admitted.
