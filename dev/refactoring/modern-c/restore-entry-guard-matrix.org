** Identity

| key | structure | rule | caveat |
|---+---+---+---|
| ticket | SessionNonce + DomainId + Generation + AttemptId | Mint outside restored graph for each new semantic invocation | Addresses or captured counters do not establish freshness |
| guard | Full TicketId + GuardSerial + OwnerGeneration | A completion witness names its immutable guard | Never look up destination from ambient currentTicket |
| semantic | Continuation invoke or admitted raw no-winding transfer | Fresh ticket before restoring; old activation retained until classified | Raw escape or unknown issuer denies optimization receipt |
| navigation | Internal dynamic-wind graft transaction | Keep same ticket through before/after navigation | Hook may escape; no completion inferred |
| scheduler | Suspended activation binding | Resume same ticket; transfer carrier under owner protocol | Processor affinity qualification remains separate |
| foreign | HostEntryId plus optional child TicketId | Nested callback new child; conversion retries retain host identity | Every ___call is not automatically a new SFUN invocation |
| transport | OperationId plus immutable TicketId and payload binding | Lost reply queries exact record | Same arguments do not identify a fresh execution |
