The submitted source unit replaces storage and address consumers for ten root
field families. It does not redesign the scheduler, collector, CFFI service,
cleanup policy or frontend optimization. Public Gambit identifiers and original
configure/Make/CI entrypoints retain their names. Each generated product must
come from the same Scheme source through the existing distribution mechanism.

The root/guard distinction matters: =na= is the existing call-arity scalar and
=heap_limit= is the existing allocator capacity guard. Both retain their unique
PSTATE storage. The root record contains =stack_limit/fp/stack_start/stack_break/
hp/r/pc/saved/type_cache/processor_scmobj=. This intermediate cut cannot rebind
an arbitrary executing context or detach its allocation/call state.

  heaps and clocks are unchanged. No tfib diagnosis is performed.

* Original failure disposition :evidence:

The fixed same-path experiment actually switches =gsi/gsi= before invoking the
unchanged official runner. The earlier parent-only binary switch was invalid:
the runner always starts =../gsi/gsi=. Its observations remain retained without
baseline attribution.

The valid12 paired schedule reproduced inactive-thread failures in5 baseline
and3 earlier candidate runs. The final ten-root candidate separately completed
408/408 original GSI cases. This establishes a pre-existing failure class and
no new observed class; it establishes neither a failure-rate improvement nor
universal absence of regression.

The official embedding test returns70 in both baseline and candidate. A
separate diagnostic view enters =___setup= and exits70 before setup returns,
with no first eval callback, in both images. Its deeper internal startup cause
remains open under the later entry/lifetime service work; the diagnostic is not
a passing official test. Earlier heartbeat warnings belong to =mix.scm= stderr,
not an established test8 cause. No official assertion or threshold is changed.

Final-source default CI-profile complete check, SMP functional roots/control
consumers and baseline failure disposition are separately reported. A default
pass cannot become whole-SMP acceptance; failed original SMP observations stay
failed in their packet and future owner route.
