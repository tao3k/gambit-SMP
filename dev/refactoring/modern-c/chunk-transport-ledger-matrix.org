** Option

| key | representation | accessRule | reuse | cost | mechanism | decision |
|---+---+---+---+---+---+---|
| unified-atomic | Preserve shared terminal/link storage | Every conflicting access and every undecided classifier uses compatible atomics | No distinct next-link node required in best case | Storage typing/alignment ABI profiles debugging and generated raw access | Convert K+C classifier loads; no speed estimate | Control; promote only after full access/storage qualification |
| split-record | Immutable in-band terminal plus independently mutable queue record | Reuse central queue gate; scanner reads immutable marker or sealed bound | Start/term/next descriptor plus pool and lifetime custody | Reserve native nodes before close; no recursive Scheme GC; dummy head and late receipts | Selected node has 3 pointer fields; at P=8 at least 24*C bytes before overhead | Main design candidate; pool/lifetime/ABI refinement still open |
