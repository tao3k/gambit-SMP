* RESEARCH One admission decision, no second registration

The following are proposed semantic cut points, not already-existing C fields.
Use current source/Thread/activation ownership and existing root/task facilities;
do not add a parallel identity registry or an ambient-thread guess.

| Cut / result | Permitted action | Roots and obligation retained |
|---+---+---|
| Before S construction and external registration | Choose existing generic lowering if any qualification is absent | Original evaluated operands/caller roots remain; no S credit and no registration to undo |
| S complete, not registered, no escaped S alias | A qualified conversion can build the generic environment/captured state from the same selected values, then transfer and ACK | Root S and destination together until complete; allocation failure leaves source intact. A known refusal is not permission to lose the call |
| Wait registration committed | Convert representation only under exclusive settlement of the same registration; keep request/result/queue/outcome unchanged | Destination becomes canonical before source relinquishes roots. Do not invoke mutex-wait! from the beginning, recapture a different wait, or enqueue again |
| Registration/conversion return lost | Query the same owner/registration/shot/generation; a known committed result is returned without replay | UNKNOWN retains source and candidate destination and excludes conflicting execution/reuse. No timeout or task-DONE shortcut establishes rollback |
| Grant already selected or user return begun | Finish its existing grant/error/user-return route | Static retry must not reinterpret a returned value as a grant, re-run service work, or duplicate dynamic-wind effects |
| Exit or exceptional transfer during conversion | Complete known root/resource transfer or retain unresolved custody | Clearing Thread.cont or resume-thunk is a transfer only when a live canonical successor and required pins exist |

Conversion has two separate publications: a fully initialized GC-scannable
root graph, then execution/registration authority. A GC may observe a complete
source plus a non-executable rooted destination, but not a descriptor selecting
half-written cells. Actual source gates/barriers, selected atomic backend and
repair authorization must order these events. A compiler poll is not a release.
After a collecting allocation reload both source and destination through their
canonical roots; never retain a decoded stack pointer across that operation.
No allocation or arbitrary hook under queue/source locks. Prepare outside locks,
revalidate identity/state inside the bounded commit, deliver effects outside.

The actual serializer also rejects Thread/tgroup/mutex/condvar structures.
An exposed wait graph containing one of these may already produce the generic
cannot-serialize error. Preserve that outcome and its permitted effects; do not
promise universal wait-continuation round-trip or invent support to earn alpha.
Current frame reconstruction includes disabled validation checks; a proposed
S admission validator must be implemented, not assumed inherited.

No universal postpublication conversion exists today. Therefore paths with
possible unsupported late exposure must use generic from the beginning until
the complete conversion/exposure protocol is qualified. This is an optimization
fallback, not rejection of valid Scheme programs. It may yield zero admission
in an open world; absence of an observed reflective call is not a proof of safety.
Unsupported or stale native metadata follows the existing rejection/error
boundary; fallback must not turn a malformed serialized object into a valid call.

* RESEARCH Qualified populations and signed net benefit

Let N=360000000 useful original flood requests; keep all target populations,
the original file/family limits, full GC and census. Let E count actual slow
completion setup episodes, M their retries and U user restorations. Do not
substitute any of those counts for another. In this selected population prove
the number of setup episodes per request before asserting alpha<=1; otherwise
alpha is a ratio, not a probability. Record per-target populations as well as total.

Partition setup choices into generic G and static entries A, with E=G+A. Let
L be entries later requiring materialization/conversion, and F extra failed or
retried conversion attempts. Entry ratio alpha_enter=A/N is not net benefit.
For the *complete original self-resume closure* credit let Q be entries proven
never to allocate that closure/equivalent environment over all relevant shots
and exposures, with Q<=A; alpha_elim=Q/N. Materialized paths and alternate facade
allocations need their own measured counts/bytes, not full Q credit. A mixed path
may retain partial savings, but only a signed resource ledger can establish it.
Capture removal has its own qualified count Kq; it is not Q or M by definition.

Each static entry must satisfy all of the following:

1. Actual private completion semantics and immutable argument facts (no general
   timeout/new-owner substitution), evaluated operand order and fallback edge.
2. Complete S/caller/check/final-return FrameSpec, debug and root maps from GVM.
3. A typed bounded retry corridor with no collecting call after raw decode;
   necessary polls and physical reserve remain in the same original budget.
4. Whole supported exposure/shot/exception scope is covered or safely converted
   without a second registration; no unknown state earns removal credit.
5. Exact module/code/layout lifetime, source freeze/repair and exit custody;
   W<R scan chooses source identity, not worker/carrier numeric coincidence.

Report both candidate and generic frame/reserve populations. The incremental
frame charge is their matched difference, including any retired generic frame;
do not charge a shared caller frame twice or assume it disappears. Physical
peak difference is max_t(candidate live/reserved bytes) minus max_t(baseline
live/reserved bytes), not the sum of independently observed component maxima.

Use disjoint measured categories: setup/corridor mutator work, exclusive GC
scan/copy/repair, conversion/exposure, and settlement/exit. Compare total CPU
and critical-path wall separately; GC pause already included in wall is not a
second saved term. Signed benefit = removed baseline work - all replacement
work for the same useful completion population. A negative term is retained,
not excluded as an unlucky fallback. Native measurements are absent in this
Goal; no numeric speedup, peak reduction or constant-space guarantee is claimed.

Illustrative threshold only: if paired measurements established gross saving
s per fully qualified episode and added net work H over the whole load, then
alpha_elim>H/(N*s), for s>0, is necessary in that simplified cost model. Other
partially optimized paths must be separately included or held equal; H can
depend on alpha, M, U and L, so this is generally an implicit inequality rather
than a predicted threshold. Current s/H/alpha are UNMEASURED. Preserve the
original workload's capacity/deadline constraints independently of net CPU.

The following is a proposed bounded record in the existing physical wait /
activation owner, not an implemented API or new global ID table. Logical S and
its shared Scheme cells remain separate from this physical record. Reserve a
record before entering a path that can publish; budget exhaustion before commit
uses the complete generic route. After commit it cannot discard the record or
claim rollback. Nested activations need their own budgeted records, not reuse
of a live parent's slot. Keep original heap/census budgets.

| Field group | Required representation and lifetime |
|---+---|
| Identity | VM lifetime, rooted logical origin, physical shot/activation generation, wait generation and conversion attempt sequence. Never a moving frame address or carrier index |
| Stable lookup | Original activation has the resident slot locator before an operation whose return can be lost; query does not depend on receiving a newly allocated token |
| Roots | Source S/ancestry, prepared generic graph and selected operands in canonical scanned homes/RootHandles; integer record identity carries no SCM liveness |
| State | Preparation/root-publication/authority-commit/ACK/retirement progress, selected outcome and known versus unknown status are distinct |

Proposed operation results distinguish PREPARED (non-executable), COMMITTED,
ALREADY_COMMITTED (same receipt), KNOWN_NOT_COMMITTED, UNKNOWN, and STALE.
These are result meanings, not names to add as another parallel runtime API.

and return rooted handles or scalars, never an unpinned raw frame pointer.
A missing record after premature reuse is not proof of NOT_COMMITTED.
No generation wrap/reuse is permitted while an old writer/query/receipt can
still be used; saturated counters refuse new admission without aliasing IDs.

** RESEARCH Same-wait transfer and query/ACK order

1. Establish source ownership and preinstalled lookup identity. Snapshot actual
   wait/outcome under existing authority, then prepare generic cells outside
   queue/source locks with complete mapped roots. GC is legal only at complete
   checkpoints; after it reload source and destination through repaired homes.
2. Publish a complete GC-visible destination while retaining source. When GC
   source admission is closed, retain rooted pending work for the next permitted
   cut or settle before that cut under its actual gate; never bypass freeze.
   A collector may see both complete graphs but no executable half-graph.
3. Under the original bounded authority/lock order revalidate current shot,
   wait, action/outcome and prepared graph revision; commit representation and
   execution target for that same registration. If a grant won, preserve it and
   use its landing, not a retry closure. No queue reinsertion, hook execution,
   GC allocation or global-source/queue lock inversion inside commit.
4. Publish the receipt after the canonical target and required authority stores
   are complete, with selected C23/GCC release/acquire or lock ordering. Query
   obtains storage pin, validates generation and reads one consistent receipt.
   Lost response repeats query of the same operation, not preparation or request
   execution. A receipt observed during GC does not itself authorize resume.
5. ACK the exact receipt idempotently only after the receiving execution/VM
   owner owns the repaired target binding. Lost ACK queries the receipt/ACK
   state. ACK permits dropping the transfer-specific source hold, not all old
   captured frames, aliases, cached actions, module refs or query storage.
6. Retire record only after closed writers, all query pins and receipt consumers,
   cached actions and active conversion readers drain, with successor roots in
   every applicable future roster. Other live Scheme aliases remain GC roots
   through their normal reachability; ACK cannot clear them. Source exit hands
   unresolved custody to existing VM/root owner, not another unknown raw Thread.

A conversion receipt commits representation/ownership, not a promise that a
pending wait can never later be granted. Keep physical outcome state/version
separate from the immutable conversion receipt; query reports a consistent
published observation and is not itself a grant or resume permit. GC repair
updates canonical root homes without changing the receipt's logical binding.
A delayed query result cannot overwrite a newer fixed outcome.

Captured logical S must not carry a reusable physical query-record capability.
A legitimate outstanding native/query capability can retain its owner record;
without a last-reader bound no finite-time retirement or bounded total memory
can be promised. Closing admission prevents new users, not existing readers.
Exhaustion can force future admissions generic but never makes already-published
unknown work disappear. This is an explicit cost/progress limit, not a timeout.

There is no current end-to-end same-wait query/ACK implementation. The actual
macros and mutex fields alone do not provide one. A release-store to a status
word cannot make separate roots coherent with source freeze or stop a cached
resume alias. The selected lock/backend and source-entry/exit adapters are
implementation prerequisites; unknown inability to stop a writer remains owned
and can block this transfer's retirement, without automatically blocking other
independent VM collection domains.

** RESEARCH Escape, multi-shot and exception responsibility

Reflection that exposes callable state must first publish one coherent alias
projection or complete the same-wait conversion. A failed allocation or lost
conversion result leaves both roots held. Existing aliases observe the same
logical cells; publishing a new facade per observer or independently copied
cells is not equivalence. Any new facade/environment allocation belongs in the
materialization ledger even when its macro name differs from ALLOC_CLO5.

A user continuation shot is not a retransmission of a physical registration.
Each admitted shot creates fresh physical authority while preserving the
specified logical captured sharing/denv/winding behavior. Retry/query within
one shot uses the same identity. Do not globally deduplicate a second shot,
replay a captured registration token or cancel another shot on ACK. GC movement
changes coordinates, not logical revision or shot identity. After restore the
source may be a heap template, active stack copy or break ancestry; decode only
from its current typed structural root.

Nonlocal exception/hook exit can occur before/after rooted preparation and
outside the bounded commit corridor. Keep normal/error cleanup edge-specific:
known not-committed preparation may be reclaimed after readers close; committed
or unknown transfer stays queryable. Before/after hooks are not idempotent
cleanup and must not be retried. The cached resume alias in the dispatcher is
an explicit last user even if Thread's action was changed by another path.
