The [[file:models/sysml/pending-cuts.sysml][collector-source-review package]]
refines delivery-plan stage 4 (workers). Its scope is the transition from
collector sweep to owner-affine cleanup and still allocation. Independent SMP
family 10 (gc-ffi) requires full writer, root and lifetime composition. This
cut alone grants zero family credit.

Sweep transfers dead foreign objects into owner cleanup custody.
=___lifecycle_drain= moves a pending object to =inflight= before the call.
A known success reclaims once. Failure keeps the debt uncertain. The sync
service calls drain after its operation loop and ignores its return value.
Processor cleanup calls the same drain and refuses teardown on failure.
The replacement must preserve these different caller outcomes explicitly.

The staged slow-path predicate references participant state absent at the
baseline. Even if that state were supplied, allocation below the deferred-word
threshold bypasses it. Use one admission decision before either branch:
an ordinary execution owner may reach the existing collect path, while an
owner servicing release effects needs a validated reservation for any still
allocation. If no reservation exists, reject before updating accounting or
publishing an object. The owner service owns outstanding credit until object
publication, verified rollback, or uncertain retention. A nested drain cannot
claim the outer in-flight object. Worker finish and owner effect completion
are separate observations.
