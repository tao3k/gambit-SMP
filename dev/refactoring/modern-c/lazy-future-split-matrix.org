** Gain

| key | benefit | cost | condition | refutation |
|---+---+---+---+---|
| materialization | N-(S+A) dynamic calls avoid eager materialization when S split and A aborted-after-allocation | N marker/check work, S copy/handoff, A abandoned allocation and K failed probes | settled cohort against eager correct task baseline; S+A<=N; unresolved allocated U must also be charged | aborted allocation counts even without successful split |
| current-noop | adds real parallel capability to a currently empty task-hook path | all marker/materialization/result machinery is additional versus current noop hooks | useful ready work and reduced span must pay for overhead | cannot claim to remove N task allocations that current hooks do not perform |
| no-steal-path | an eligible unclaimed occurrence needs no separate parent/placeholder materialization in this model | existing GVM register saves/frame work plus new marker checks remain | no successful claim, no allocated aborted claim | no-split alone does not imply no allocation |
| root-reuse | reuse precise frame decoders and context root enumeration | new private-copy/queue/restore/result homes and slot partition proof | descriptors and moving updates qualified across actual collector phases | a second independent scanner or stale copied pointers erases correctness |
| queue-reuse | published parent can reuse runnable-context scheduler consumer | new materialization adapter and split-specific ready/grant states | existing thread identity, locks, pinning and denv restored correctly | thread stealing alone cannot split active frames |
| continuation | after-value suffix can replay without creating a second child for the same admitted occurrence | per-shot execution/root/wind work remains; fresh pre-spawn creates real new work | capture point classified and semantics preserved | memoizing an effectful suffix or banning legal continuation is not a benefit |
| batching | Erlang suggests amortized queued transfer and bounded lock hold time | batch root ownership and contention overhead | single-task proof composes to batches without duplicate grant | no measured or proved batching benefit here |
