This is the complete root-to-worker-to-exit replacement cut for the mandatory
modern C direction. It refines the existing [[file:smp-factor-conditions.org][ffi/work/exit dependencies]] and
[[file:../../architecture/runtime/control/frame-alias-contract.org][shared-frame contract]]. It neither implements independent workers nor claims
that the current copying collector has become generational. The [[file:models/sysml/root-worker-exit.sysml][native SysML model]]
owns the clauses, prerequisites, witnesses, learning records and native cases.
[[file:../../modeling/ascent/root-worker-exit.rules.scm][Actual ASCENT rules]] distinguish source location, supplied refinement, upstream
learning and native acceptance. No new QNT or Lean model is created.

The remaining coupling is larger than a participant array lookup. Setup binds
execution context, participant and collector by the same processor index. Strong,
weak and cleanup phases update that indexed participant. Stealing and termination
use the carrier-derived population. Copy allocation still uses context memory;
the GC driver supplies context refill, barriers, overflow delivery and resume.
Removing only the synchronous assertion would remove a real safety check without
establishing independent work ownership.

=___garbage_collect_pstate= has two direct source call sites in =setup.c=: the
resize capacity branch and the synchronous operation executor. The public
=___garbage_collect= packages its request through =on_all_processors=. Those
routes must migrate together, including merged still-capacity requests, root
freeze and result consumption; neither can remain an independent GC driver.

The two direct =___run= callers are the spawned-processor wrapper and initial
module startup. The former reloads the rooted thunk after the synchronization
loop; the latter enters through the generic marker path. The rooted marker
endpoint now reloads its incoming root after allocation. Its raw wrapper passes
the address of a C local without registering that local: these are different
admission guarantees. Generated SFUN/CFUN and nested callbacks additionally reach
the lower call machinery and must obey the same root/activation boundary.
An individual initial-module thunk may have permanent/nonmoving provenance;
that classification must be established, rather than declaring every raw call
movable or treating the raw signature as a registered-root guarantee.

RC registration writes the current context chain, while =mark_rc= scans the
explicit supplied context. Therefore origin ownership and scanner lifetime must
survive carrier withdrawal; transferring an RC pointer without transferring the
whole rooted domain and reader responsibility does not suffice.

** Identity, publication and completion

RootOrigin identifies a VM-owned root domain with a nonreused incarnation.
RoundMember identifies that origin's inclusion and frozen view for one round.
RootTask identifies an actual scan obligation. CollectorWorker identifies a
temporary execution agent. HostCarrier identifies an OS thread and instrumentation
context. Equality of any two numeric slots supplies none of the other identities.
Slots may be bounded and reused only after actual readers, tasks and native
borrowers are closed; unresolved incarnations deny reuse rather than wrap.

The round gate serializes admission against close. Writers publish a complete
frozen view under that gate after ordinary writes and native borrows have stopped.
Release/acquire queue publication precedes a task's claim. An origin included in
this round cannot return and mutate its root slots while an unclaimed or active
task can still scan them. Late admissions either obtain a complete inclusion
decision or retain their parent/source roots outside the closed set. They never
wait for GC while holding a root writer or native reentry permit.

A root task finishes after its authoritative root slots are repaired and its
children are durably accounted for. It does not finish the strong phase. The
round coordinator advances only after all relevant producers are closed and
queued, running, private-copy, Tail and descendant debts have custody/closure
appropriate to that transition. Worker handoff proves a new custodian, not that
the work has already completed. Weak/finalizer/hash-table tasks have their own
round authorization; per-origin phase stores cannot stand in for that authority.

Plain C payload slots are accessed under frozen or exclusive ownership. Atomic
revision checks alone do not legalize racing non-atomic root or frame accesses.
Condition-variable predicates and their transitions use the same gate. A failed
claim has no root-write authority. An unknown claim stays recorded in-flight.

** Worker allocation and context restoration

The collector's arena owns destination spans, publication frontier, first-field
Tail and refund debt independently of a stopped mutator's allocation tips.
Every collector-reachable use of context allocator macros, section refill,
frame copying, still counters, tospace interpretation and actlog/consistency
helpers needs classification and migration. Replacing =work->context= with a
different pointer name leaves this coupling intact.

The pre-flip physical plan accounts live copy demand, contiguous maximum request,
packing loss and first-resume reserves across the actual worker and origin sets.
Any shared accounting uses one canonical ledger: worker reserved space and the
same context resume interval cannot both count as free. Known reusable worker
tails may reduce peak only if committed to the joint plan before that peak.
Post-round refunds alone do not establish a lower whole-round peak.

A context resumes after acquiring its whole-round seal, immutable outcome and
physical grant, then reloading every authoritative root/address. This preserves
Gambit's lazy reached-frame restoration, multi-shot control and Scheme winding.
It does not migrate a live native C stack or resurrect an expired native origin.
The existing FrameSpec/alias and whole-round capacity source refinements remain
external prerequisites of this complete cut, not newly proved lemmas here.

** Exit, failure and physical reclamation

Separate execution admission closure, actual heap-write closure, task/root
handoff, result delivery, native-user closure and physical recycle. A known
frozen root domain and safely stopped worker can transfer to VM custody even
when native-handle join is unknown. Another worker may continue scanning that
domain. An unknown writer that may still touch the shared moving heap cannot be
isolated merely by removing it from the member set; the affected VM can remain
blocked. A healthy separate VM is a different progress observation.

Partial creation failure keeps unpublished origins and grants owned by the VM
until their disposition is known. Donor rejection keeps the donor responsible;
unknown transfer keeps both records but only one execution authorization. No
timeout, logical exiting flag or absent queue item proves physical stop.

Before deferring a foreign release, effects, affinity, rooted payload and budget
must already be admitted to the round. A cleanup routine cannot create an
unscanned moving-root dependency after strong closure. Unsupported releases
remain outside delayed admission. Eligible owner-dependent work is handed to
rooted VM custody and executed only when the owner is permitted to run, avoiding
the GC-waits-owner/owner-waits-GC cycle.

Only known final native users, transferred/dead roots, discharged scanner pins
and qualified reader grace authorize physical freeing. Managed grace cannot
cover an unmanaged C borrower. Host cleanup may release an OS handle after its
native receipt; that does not implicitly free the VM-owned root domain or arena.

Let R be admitted root origins, W collector workers, and J root/VM/copy tasks.
Independent workers remove the requirement W=R; they do not make useful work
unbounded. Scanning the same roots is still required. A task claim per origin
adds O(R) claim/dispatch work per admitted root kind; private arenas add O(W)
control records, plus actual interval/queue/descendant storage. This is not a
byte bound for live graphs or unknown native resources.

For the same useful workload, net elapsed benefit requires avoided mandatory
carrier participation, imbalance, redundant work and qualified blocking to exceed
new root/claim/publication/closure/grace costs on the critical path. R/W/J are
unmeasured here; no ideal speedup is reported as achieved. A serial scheduler,
shared global claim bottleneck, unresolved shared writer, incorrect copy capacity
or broken continuation behavior falsifies the intended benefit. Physical peak
includes simultaneous copy and context restoration reserves plus retained debt.
