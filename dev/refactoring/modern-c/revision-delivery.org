the delivery order. Modern C reconstruction is the programme; repair9 is one
source of failure/cost evidence. The next selection is startup preparation and
member publication, with unchanged test8 success as a required acceptance case.
Do not add another wait/service optimization while this foundation is unresolved.

The plan separates current delivery priority from architectural prerequisites.
Header extraction, generated products, build tooling, CI and runtime prototypes
have separate owners; no file-pattern group is an automatic submission tree.
Existing published leaves remain published, with their original scoped evidence.
Unverified index/working changes remain proposals until their own bounded source
and required tests qualify. This planning update changes no runtime or commit.
