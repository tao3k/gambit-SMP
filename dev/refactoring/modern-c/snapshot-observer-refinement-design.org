* RESEARCH Architectural decision
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: snapshot-observer-refinement
:AUTHORITY: GambitSnapshotObserverRefinement
:STATUS: scoped-formal; source-C11-and-performance-open
:END:

Modern generated C remains the required direction. This study extends
[[file:derivation-receipt-environment-design.org][the joint ledger/receipt reference protocol]].
It separates native snapshot storage, executable code/OFD lifetime, and terminal
receipt storage. A successful task or an idle worker is not a grace-period proof
for any of those three domains. No runtime code, build recipe or native test is
introduced. Current source is rebound by hash; predecessor receipts stay frozen.

The single immutable LedgerHead is retained as a linearizable specification, not
a mandated global CAS bottleneck for every GC chunk. Current Gambit already has
per-processor chunk queues. A modernization that centralizes all their traffic
must justify the extra contention; the mere presence of lock-free CAS is no gain.

* RESEARCH Source and literature anchors
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: snapshot-source-basis
:END:

Current mem.c:end_heap_chunk links initialized chunk content and changes the
per-processor tail with barriers and queue lock ownership. Local and stolen chunks
are scanned after head advancement and unlock. mark_reachable_from_marked joins
termination through scan_termination_mutex/workers_count. These are distinct queue,
in-flight scan and phase responsibilities, not the proposed task ledger.
Stack/captured-frame scanning reads decoded descriptors and trailing GC-map words;
returning from the decoder is not the final OFD access. os_dyn.c has global unload,
not a proved old-cohort grace period. Native snapshot allocation/reclamation is
also not automatically part of Scheme still-object sweep or moving-root tracking.

[[https://www.eecg.utoronto.ca/~amza/ece1747h/papers/hazard_pointers.pdf][Michael's hazard-pointer paper]]
provides the protect-before-dereference and deferred reclamation mechanism studied
here. We do not transfer its performance results to Gambit. Our SC reference
ordering and concrete admission/certificate rules are design proposals requiring
source and C11 refinement, not claims that the paper proves this runtime.

[[https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~tabrown/debra/fullpaper.pdf][Brown's reclamation study]]
explains why a stalled participant can prevent ordinary epoch reclamation and
motivates separating allocation/reclamation policy from data-structure semantics.
Its signaling solution is not automatically safe for Gambit signals, GC or FFI.

[[https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.11347][Kim, Brown and Singh's batch-free study]]
shows allocator interactions can make large freeing batches costly. Consequently
commit batching and free batching are separate decisions here. We claim no
allocator behavior, tail-latency ratio or throughput result for Darwin/Gambit.

* RESEARCH Snapshot pin and reclamation protocol
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: snapshot-pin-order
:MODEL: models/quint/snapshot_observer.qnt
:END:

The directory and slot roster have independent stable lifetimes. A snapshot
identity is (directory generation, snapshot generation, address), not an address
alone. The reference reader loads identity, publishes it in its registered slot,
then reloads head identity. It dereferences only after equality revalidation and
retains its slot through the final access. A failed validation discards the bare
identity; it does not decode the old pointer first.

The remover detaches the old identity from head, records it on a retired list,
then scans the complete stable slot roster. A reader publishing a pin AFTER a
clear scan is not necessarily unsafe: validation must then fail because head has
already changed. Conversely, a reader that validated the old head before removal
must remain visible until its final dereference. The bounded model deliberately
allows late publication after the scan; it never grants that reader validation.

For the first reference proof, head load/validation/removal, pin publication and
scan observations use a sequentially consistent metadata ordering. Let p be pin
publication, v old-head validation, r removal and s a scan that misses that still-held
pin. Required relations would be p<v<r<s<p, a cycle. Lean proves that arithmetic
contradiction. The proof assumes no early pin clear, stable membership and no ABA;
it is NOT a full C11 program proof. Release/acquire on separate head and hazard
objects cannot simply be substituted: an acquire need not read the relevant
release. The negative weak-scan control injects such an unqualified observation.

[[https://www9.open-std.org/JTC1/SC22/WG14/www/docs/n1570.pdf][WG14 N1570]],
5.1.2.4 and 7.17, supplies the C11 ordering basis. This research's SC metadata
candidate is deliberately stronger than an unproved relaxed variant. Actual
compiler operations, fences, lock freedom and source data-race freedom remain
unqualified. A weaker protocol requires its own valid execution-graph proof.

| Boundary | Required custodian / condition | Refusal or unknown outcome |
|----------+--------------------------------+----------------------------|
| slot registration | resident directory owner admits participant before roster cut | unregistered FFI/late participant cannot read payload |
| pin publication | reader owns stable slot; no pointer dereference yet | failed validation retries against current generation |
| final use | task/reader still owns snapshot and any separate code/root grant | exception/continuation cannot silently clear or lose the grant |
| detached snapshot | retired-list owner retains immutable storage | failed scan or unknown transfer retains storage |
| freeing | completed ordered scan and no validated holder for generation | unknown roster/ABA/lifetime refuses reclaim |
| directory retirement | closes slot admission and drains registered accessors | freeing code does not free directory or hazard slots |

The QNT models one old snapshot, one reader slot and one generation transition.
It does not model address reuse. The no-ABA obligation explicitly requires no
address/generation reuse through the grace period or a proven tagged-reuse scheme.
A stalled validated reader can keep its protected snapshot; cancellation must
complete final use or transfer an explicit record. No epoch counter or elapsed
time alone revokes an arbitrary raw pointer. Scheme values in native records need
RootRegistry/GC-compatible slots independently of metadata pins.

* RESEARCH Receipt observers and executable retirement
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: snapshot-three-lifetimes
:END:

Closing task publication is distinct from closing receipt lookup. Already admitted
queries retain observer leases even after lookup admission closes. Query code first
uses the resident directory and identity, then acquires a valid observer lease;
it does not dereference a raw terminal-record pointer and register afterwards.
Terminal records survive pending delivery, admitted/unknown queries and duplicate
TaskId replay. A fresh request after the observer gate cut must fail admission.

A data-only receipt may outlive executable code ONLY under a DetachCertificate.
It certifies stable numeric/copied result data, resident query/exception code,
no saved old return/continuation/catcher, no old label/OFD dereference, no borrowed
Scheme value, and no callback/function pointer back to the cohort. The certificate
covers future admitted observers as well as currently active ones. exec=0 cannot
issue it. Without detachment, BOTH observer admission and existing observers must
close/drain before code retirement. With detachment, code can retire while an
observer remains; receipt storage and directory remain separately owned.

The certifyDetached QNT action is an explicit external semantic assumption. The
Lean witness proves that the permissions are logically compatible, not that any
existing Gambit consumer can sign such a certificate. Unknown in this bounded
model denotes original pending task-delivery status and cannot be newly asserted
after it has been settled. Additional native query/finalizer uncertainties need
separate domain-specific records, not one unscoped global flag.

Old code release must occur from resident completion code after native epilogues,
catcher restoration, GC/root use and possible continuation resumption no longer
access the cohort. Snapshot pin clearance is not an executable-unload permission.
The scope of the code permission guard assumes all independent root/FFI/inspection
producers have already closed; complete source inventories remain open.

* RESEARCH Global CAS cost and batch refinement
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: snapshot-contention-model
:END:

Count mandatory shared commits before estimating speed. For D child tasks, the
reference reserves, publishes, claims and completes each child: 4D successful
head commits, plus parent/admission/receipt operations G. A attempts include failed
CAS retries, so candidate construction/cleanup and pin/scan costs depend on A,
not merely successful commits. No actual retry rate or machine instruction count
is known. Serializing all updates on one head introduces a critical path whose
length is the sum of those commits' service costs, regardless of core count.
Overall time has lower bounds from total work divided by available processors,
semantic dependency span and that shared-head path. Their constants are unmeasured.

For batch size b>=1 and k legal batchable child phases (0<=k<=4), the conditional
commit count is (4-k)D+k*ceil(D/b)+G. The child-phase reduction is exactly
k*(D-ceil(D/b)); this is NOT an elapsed-time percentage. Claims and completions
are not assumed batchable when readiness/completion times differ. Waiting for a
batch can increase span, retained bytes and cancellation latency.

The maintained Lean algebra proves two disjoint reservations commute and an
explicit two-item batch equals their serial composition. Admission additionally
requires same cohort/schema, distinct identities, stable membership, preserved
per-ticket roots/debts/receipts and no intervening dependent reads/effects. Either
commit all accepted members atomically, or publish a precise mixed result vector.
One member's receipt is never proof that the whole batch succeeded. A batch lease
can cover descendants only if it owns all private/queued/running work and observers.

Free scheduling is independent: bounded incremental freeing can reduce bursts,
but introduces a deferred-free custodian and backlog. Removing a snapshot from the
retired list before its deallocation responsibility is accepted would repeat the
same ownership gap. No allocator/tail-latency improvement is asserted.

* RESEARCH Shards and a global retirement cut
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: snapshot-shard-refinement
:MODEL: models/quint/shard_retirement_cut.qnt
:END:

Shard local ownership where semantics permit; keep a stable cohort generation and
explicit global producer cut. Transferring work reserves target ownership BEFORE
source release. The intermediate overlap counts the same logical unit twice,
conservatively. Lean subtracts that overlap to recover logical ownership and
proves conservation. There is no moment when both shards disclaim the work.
A sealed target rejects transfer; source remains responsible and must finish
locally, retry an admissible target, or transfer to a stable coordinator.

The two-shard QNT permits either direction, private transfer, target reservation,
source release, abort, local completion and final seal. A shard can seal only with
closed independent admission, zero local debt and no in-flight transfer. Incoming
transfer to a sealed shard is forbidden. Both seals, zero debts and zero transfers
are required before retirement. No sum of independently sampled local zero values
is a global cut. Unknown response retains the transfer intent/source reservation;
full lost-response reconciliation composes with the predecessor TaskId contract
and is not modeled as a new successful migration here.

Each shard action above is still an abstract transition. A production split across
target-ledger CAS and source-intent update must use idempotent transfer TaskId and
recoverable target receipts. A lost target response keeps source debt, even when
the target already reserved. The composed abstract overlap is determined by actual
canonical ticket states, not only the sender's local knowledge. This additional
unknown-response/observation relation is OPEN; the six finite shard histories do
not prove such a distributed implementation. Target count and receipt can commit
in one local immutable snapshot; that does not atomically update another shard.

Production refinement must additionally establish complete shard membership, no
unregistered root/publisher, inherited derivation rules, cross-shard GC/root/FFI
dependencies, queue/cancellation semantics and final observer drain. The finite
model does not prove those premises or unrestricted allocation of new tasks.
With S shards and local successful loads H_i, serialization is governed by the
most loaded shard plus transfer and cut coordination, not automatically by H/S.
Cache placement, memory bandwidth, imbalance, snapshot allocation and transfer
frequency can defeat the expected gain. Keep current distributed chunk queues
unless replacing them has an independently demonstrated benefit.

* RESEARCH JSON and formal evidence chain
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: snapshot-json-chain
:END:

The actual snapshot QNT typed IR is used to generate guard/update expressions for
publishPin, validatePin and freeReceipt. Lean checks each pair against maintained
PinFlat semantics. This is syntax-level correspondence for those actual expressions;
no complete protocol-history or C11 simulation is claimed. The separate shard
model has bounded histories and conditional transfer algebra, not a generated
full transition-system correspondence.

The pinned map JSON still supplies 91 selected occurrence/declaration pairs through
the existing rejecting gate. Its generated finite-table consistency certificate
is reused in the new scope. environmentNoFabrication proves every lookup result
is a row of the finite table. environmentExactDomain combines that converse with
the earlier row-to-function theorem. It closes a conditional finite-domain relation,
not raw bytes-to-authentic-bindings correctness. Canonical declaration, type, scope,
selected-domain completeness and field-slot extraction remain checked/trusted
boundaries. Row deletion/target change and strict manifest reordering controls
remain distinct from semantic proofs; unique-row reordering need not alter lookup.

SysML gives typed accountable/counterpart component references, dependencies and
exact model scopes. ASCENT requires the shard receipt as well as pin/Lean/actual-AST
receipts and predecessor source obligations; missing certificates cannot be
promoted to full source or platform qualification. Org matrices and D2 edges are
native SysML projections. [[file:snapshot-observer-refinement-matrix.org][The net-benefit matrix]]
records symbolic work counts and conditions, with open source/C11/performance gates.

* RESEARCH Migration and retirement conditions
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: snapshot-migration
:END:

Define resident directory and slot ownership; prove stable membership and final-use
boundaries; distinguish executable/root and data-only receipt capabilities; qualify
SC pin/refusal behavior; retain the joint ledger as a specification; then refine
legal batching and shard transfers under a global cut. Only migrated consumers
may retire implicit lifetime assumptions. No existing FIFO, GC phase counter,
root scanner, C macro or global unloading path is retired by this research.
