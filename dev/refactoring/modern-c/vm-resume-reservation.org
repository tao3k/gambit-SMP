* Observed gap in the first transaction candidate
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: vm-resume-zero-budget
:AUTHORITY: GambitVMResumeReservation
:STATUS: SOURCE-IMPLEMENTED; FRESH-NATIVE-QUALIFICATION-PENDING; COMMIT-BLOCKED
:END:

The first physical-grant candidate completes its official two-pass bootstrap,
but its map compiler times out and subsequent lanes fail with heap overflow.
No native pass is inherited. A read-only LLDB census of that exact executable
shows 22 sections, all 22 assigned, 11 members, and overflow reserve 204,808
words. Global free budget is therefore -204,808 words. The ten new contexts
enter collection with heap limits four words behind hp and stack limits
24 words ahead of fp. These are actual observations, not the original
six-section exhaustion and not a confirmed claim of a scheduler deadlock.

* One joint capacity contract
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: vm-resume-joint-floor
:END:

The actual =prepare_mem_pstate= consumer computes =avail=max(free/2,0)=,
then gives the stack =avail/2=. Physical ownership alone cannot qualify a
startup grant. The same capacity decision must leave a logical allowance:

#+BEGIN_EXAMPLE
W = 131072 words per physical section
F = MSECTION_FUDGE = 8194 words in this profile
O = normal_overflow_reserve = 204808 words
R = ceil((O + 4*F) / W) = 2 VM-unassigned sections
new capacity >= old assigned + 2*new contexts + R
post-GC floor >= copied assigned + actual fudge-restoration sections + R
only startup pairs become assigned; R stays on the VM's free list
free >= 4*F => stack allowance >= F, subject to its own physical limit
#+END_EXAMPLE

The reserve comes from the existing overflow policy and actual subdivision
algorithm. It is not a larger default heap setting. Contraction after every GC
must preserve the same allowance; keeping it only during the first growth
would recreate the failure at the next collection. Caller allocation bound B,
arbitrary FFI/foreign allocation and complete C refinement remain obligations.

* Current boundary and cost
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: vm-resume-cost
:END:

The two additional physical sections carry two MiB of payload on this profile,
before allocator metadata. The original six-section VM must cover two assigned
sections, twenty startup sections and two free resume sections: 24 in this
observed state. Usable existing capacity is subtracted before requesting more
physical memory. This is a capacity floor, not peak RSS, an arbitrary performance
budget, or an extra cost charged against a correct modern C baseline.
