* Physical and root conservation :contract:
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: STARTUP-CONSERVATION
:END:

Let =A= be the assigned physical prefix, =N= new contexts, =S= section words,
=F= the existing allocation fudge, and =O= the existing normal overflow reserve.
The startup grant condition is

#+BEGIN_EXAMPLE
capacity >= A + 2*N + ceil((O + 4*F)/S)
#+END_EXAMPLE

The final term follows =prepare_mem_pstate=: half of approximate free space is
split between stack and heap. It is unassigned VM resume headroom, not another
context's pair. In the recorded64-bit profile =S=131072, F=8194, O=204808=, hence
two sections. These quantities come from the existing policy; they are not a
new heap default or permission to exceed a finite configured cap. Already
assigned copy/restore ranges cannot be freed just because a cap is smaller;
overflow must remain an explicit outcome. Whole pre-copy packing is later work.

Conditional preservation argument:

1. Under the old stopped cohort, allocator growth is checked for its actual
   returned count, including partial malloc success. The whole2N linked suffix
   is validated before any slot grant or counter charge. Pairs are successive
   non-overlapping physical sections. Capacity metadata may move; individual
   section addresses stay stable under the existing allocator contract.
2. Setup contains no Scheme collection/callback. All grant counters are charged
   once, and setup consumes only its pair. The coordinator's C fields have one
   writer until creation/commit. An out-of-contract native interruption is not
   an atomic multi-store transaction and does not authorize helper takeover.
3. Each caller RC cell protects the pending request independently of its copied
   sync record. The child cell is registered/borrowed before create. Platform
   thread creation publishes immutable arguments. Gate unlock/lock orders
   RUN/ABORT; children cannot touch TLS, GC or Scheme while PREPARING.
4. Membership commit requires every setup and create result. The existing
   trailing sync-loop collective covers the enlarged set. No unmatched extra
   barrier, yield-count readiness inference or wait for scheduler-thunk return
   is introduced. The child acquires use of its cell under the gate, but the
   VM slot retains the custody receipt through synchronization and marker
   construction. Servicing a shrink can exit that C stack before either returns.
5. Marker construction reads the traced cell after its allocating operation.
   The still marker then owns the Scheme value. Only then are borrow and RC
   ownership returned, after removing the VM receipt under the gate. Catcher
   cleanup uses volatile custody across supported setjmp/longjmp paths. A
   thread_exit bypasses the catcher: the known-join coordinator releases the
   still-registered startup root. An expired external C stack is not resumed.
6. On known failure, ABORT precedes joins; cleanup/refund follows every known
   successful join. Logical assigned/stack/cursor charges revert exactly.
   Newly allocated free sections may remain cached: this is not a physical
   malloc refund or a lower peak claim. Unknown join keeps roots, grants and
   metadata, refuses reuse and GC in the owning VM, and receives no successful
   reclamation claim. Unmanaged owner loss and composed multi-VM global cleanup
   remain explicit later admission duties.

Counterexamples are concrete: enough aggregate words without distinct pairs;
future count published before a failed pthread create; a copied raw thunk used
after GC; partial setup cleaned through the wrong =ps=; refund after unknown
join; and inferring scheduler readiness from count/yields. Each invalidates a
different premise. A guard reporting these states alone would not replace the
old responsibility.
