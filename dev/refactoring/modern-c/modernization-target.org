** What was wrong with the preceding direction
The MC-SCOPE commit implements a real packed gate and initializes fixed records.
It does not replace a generated entry, GC root consumer, collector work owner,
VM membership protocol or native-effect lifetime. The patched runtime already
has admission CAS and retained cleanup. Merely adding another gate alongside
that code is not a fundamental solution and has no demonstrated net benefit.

The candidate adds 512 record bytes per current 64-bit VM, zero retired paths,
and finite role/actor/generation limits. Those limits must not become arbitrary
restrictions on Scheme continuations or supported native entry. The target model
therefore evaluates its architecture-adoption predicate to false. It is neither
rolled into the qualified runtime nor counted as 9/12. A concrete source commit
and a justified architectural selection are different claims.

* Lessons, with the Gambit choice made explicit
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: modernization-lessons
:END:
** Parallelism is a heap, participation and FFI design
The [[https://kcsrk.info/papers/retro-parallel_icfp_20.pdf][ICFP parallelism paper]], especially sections 1.3, 3.4 and 4.3-4.4, compares
minor-collector choices by their consequences for C reads, safepoints and
coordination. Its parallel STW minor design avoids the new read-barrier contract.
This is evidence for evaluating the entire interface, not a mandate to copy its
major collector. The paper explicitly cites Feeley's polling work for safepoints.
Gambit's own compiler/runtime ideas are assets to amplify, not a historical shell
to replace with another language's architecture.

The checked OCaml source at =46e1c324fb9e9f21f52c9cc3399020f7ef8b9b91=
separately shows participating-domain minor promotion and cleanup
(=runtime/minor_gc.c:886-934=), domain/backup-thread participation
(=runtime/domain.c=), and blocking leave/reenter hooks
(=runtime/signals.c:147-205=). These are current-source observations, not claims
that every detail is the 2020 paper's implementation.

Our design position: separate execution, OS carrier, GC participation, collector
work and native-resource ownership. Use context-local nursery allocation and a
parallel stopped-mutator minor cut with precise roots and remembered writes.
Keep mature-space policy behind an explicit heap/phase interface: moving versus
nonmoving mature space and concurrent major marking remain decisions requiring
Gambit-specific evidence. Copying GC alone is not generational GC.

** Effects require a control architecture
The [[https://kcsrk.info/papers/retro-concurrency_pldi_21.pdf][PLDI effects paper]], sections 4-5, specifies alternating language/C stacks,
callbacks, exceptions and resumptions together. Its continuation implementation
is intentionally one-shot. That constraint cannot be imposed on Gambit.

Our design position: running, captured and resumed Scheme computation share one
precise frame/root contract. Multi-shot continuations remain first class;
persistent or copy-on-write captured frames are evaluated as representations.
One-shot specialization requires compiler/use evidence. A future effect-handler
service composes with these semantics instead of replacing call/cc or silently
forbidding supported continuation behavior at native boundaries.

** Recover semantic intent before native lowering
[[https://www.iro.umontreal.ca/~feeley/papers/FeeleyMillerLFP90.pdf][Feeley and Miller's PVM paper]], sections 1-2, treats the VM as cooperation
between Scheme optimization and target information, including calls, registers,
closures and primitives. Our design extends that principle to complete native
contracts, rather than asking C macros or a Zig wrapper to rediscover meaning.

Scheme completes FrameSpec, PointId, ModuleSpec, EffectSpec and AllocationSpec.
Modern C realizes layouts, checkpoints and services mechanically. Target layout
is parameterized without duplicating semantic decisions. LLVM/MLIR can be
independent consumers/services at an appropriate semantic level. Zig is a
separate optional realization decision and supplies no missing GC semantics.

* The six coupled replacements
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: modernization-replacements
:END:
#+BEGIN: sysml-view :kind modernization-target :source models/sysml/modernization-target.sysml
See [[file:modernization-target-matrix.org][the generated six-decision matrix]] for exact source anchors, replacement,
retirement, proof obligations, benefits and added costs. This view is not an
implementation checklist for adding six wrappers.
#+END:

1. Contract compiler: move semantic/frame/effect decisions out of distributed C
   emission macros into a Scheme-owned normalized contract. Generate C functions,
   metadata and ABI manifests. Keep target-portability macros where they serve
   real target variation; eliminate duplicate semantic logic, not every macro.
2. Execution/participation: VM owns membership and heap epochs; execution context
   owns Scheme state; a participant publishes a precise stopped/frozen root view;
   collector work owns its queues/cursors independently of PSTATE/OS-thread death.
3. Heap: nursery allocation, remembered mutation, parallel minor promotion,
   survivor capacity and major collection are explicit services. Continuation,
   denv, initialization and FFI writers participate in the same barrier contract.
4. Control: one frame/root interpretation survives capture, promotion, repeated
   resumption, dynamic-wind and exceptional exits. The scheduler operates on
   explicit suspended computations rather than opaque C-stack ownership.
5. Native effects/resources: generated root-safe entry precedes allocation;
   blocking publishes a view and reacquires/reloads on return. VM-owned outcomes
   and effect-classified release tasks survive carrier exits without requiring
   an owner callback inside the collector's critical phase.
6. Build: semantic module artifacts, runtime ABI and native objects form a
   dependency/snapshot graph with phase readiness. Interface construction code
   can be AOT-reused while context instances remain fresh. This must eliminate
   actual repeated expansion and compilation, including the macOS C-parser
   cost; changing the compiler command does not establish this result.

All six use the canonical RootCut, ControlRoots, Participation, WorkClosure,
ReleaseDrain, OutcomeLifetime, PlanAbi, GeneratedCheckpoint and ArtifactClosure
interfaces. A module model alone is insufficient: the source transition must
preserve the preconditions expected by its consumers.

* System-level evidence before adoption
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: modernization-proof-boundary
:END:
The [[file:map-multishot-composition.org][joint map/multi-shot study]] now gives a concrete source witness,
seven boundaries, ten shared flows and native-SysML-driven ASCENT obligations.
Its finite custody model and conditional laws are deliberately narrower than
the twelve whole-source composition obligations here. Neither upgrades those
larger scopes or admits the inactive scope-gate candidate.

Use one Scheme =map= computation that captures a continuation and crosses an
admitted native callback as a composed witness: allocate/capture, promote, mutate
legal frame/denv state, block, collect with an independent worker, reenter, resume
more than once, and close resources under exceptional exit. This is the target
composition to prove and eventually execute, not a new test run this turn.

QNT must model GC/control/native/scheduler interleavings over the shared contracts.
Lean must prove frame/root coverage, remembered-edge completeness, publication
and capacity conservation and semantic simulation. ASCENT must reject receipt
scope widening from an isolated gate or model to this whole witness. The
architecture model references existing formal fragments, marks all six composed
scopes open and binds them through the actual DSL.

The baseline 8/12 has four distinct remaining contracts. Lifecycle requires real
failure recovery and old-handler quiescence; GC/FFI requires complete exact-source
inventory; Gerbil requires a source-causal repair and complete downstream work;
release performance requires unchanged matched whole-consumer campaigns. None
is solved by declaring a new architecture, and the unknown Gerbil fault is not
automatically diagnosed as a collector bug. See the
[[file:../../architecture/runtime/gc/write-authority-gate-matrix.org][source/milestone matrix]]. Their original tests remain mandatory acceptance
constraints after replacement, not the shape of its implementation.

* Benefit and cost are architectural admission conditions
The target model tracks eliminated responsibilities and work. Minor collection
may reduce scanning to young reachable objects, remembered edges and roots, but
adds write barriers, promotion/copying, synchronization and remembered storage.
Skipping mature continuation graphs is conditional on complete mutation coverage.
Incremental builds save only actions whose full dependency/input/consumer contract
is stable; artifact checks and fresh context state still cost work.

Net gain requires avoided scans, repeated frame/root choreography and duplicate
expansion/compilation to exceed the added barriers, coordination, validation and
retained-space cost. Peak memory includes simultaneously live old/new roots,
copy/promotion reserve, context restoration space and pending native resources;
transferable intervals are counted once. No paper's speedup, assumed allocation
rate or artificial person-day estimate supplies that inequality for Gambit.

Retirement is part of the result: old implicit PSTATE collector ownership,
duplicated root/frame decisions, per-entry native cleanup protocols and repeated
build work must actually disappear from the admitted profile. If every old owner
remains and the new layer merely tracks it, the modernization case fails.
