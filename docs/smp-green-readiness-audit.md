# SMP Full-Green Readiness Audit

## Conclusion

The complete SMP/multi-VM/threaded-VM goal is not qualified. There is real
source repair, bounded-model and native diagnostic progress, but no single
current candidate has completed the full acceptance chain. No defensible
overall completion percentage or remaining-time estimate follows from these
receipts. Standard thread file coverage is not whole-runtime coverage.

The parent and new candidate identities are different. Parent native successes
must not be credited to the new build. Diagnostic O0 build times do not measure
production throughput, and archived model PASS does not admit a runtime.

## Concrete Distance

1. Establish the checked producer-to-review-to-suite continuation without
   importing an unadmitted archived binary, predicting hashes or resetting
   budgets. The bounded handoff model is checked; the API is not implemented.
2. Bind the recovered original failure receipts and completion fixture changes,
   then execute the entire standard thread inventory in both modes on the new
   candidate. Preserve the original assertions and historical failures.
3. Close the whole GC/lifecycle composition requirements with source-bound
   models and actual stress/fault/forced-GC acceptance, not only local schedules.
4. Complete two-pass bootstrap and full upstream native tests on the same
   identified candidate, followed by complete Gerbil correctness.
5. Qualify production performance and reliability with all three flags enabled,
   and finish the complementary Lean obligations through the shared driver.

These are substantial acceptance stages, not merely two remaining tests.
Stages can reveal new source defects; passing a stage does not imply that the
later stages will pass. No fixed number of days or overall percentage is
supported by the current evidence.

## Audit Freshness And Limitations

Direct inspection of `evidence/research-audit.json` now finds three Reflection issues:
the notification composition has eight requirements and 25 conditions; the
historical interrupt composition has three requirements and seven conditions;
the new lifetime composition has three requirements and six conditions. Together
that is 14 non-closed requirements and 38 conditions, with overlapping scopes,
not 38 independent bugs or a count of distinct remaining repairs.
These are inventory counts, not a fresh native Datalog graph result. The
37 entries in `evidence/native-failures.json` are retained historical failures
across different candidates, not 37 proven bugs in the newest candidate.

Some Reflection prose still predates the combined interrupt fixture execution
and later thread coverage. Such stale prose must not erase later receipts, but
later diagnostic observations cannot silently discharge formal conditions.
The preceding matched framework graph passed 770 tests on its prior inputs.
After the four new bundles were registered, a fresh graph ran all 770 tests
and was DENY with one historical-registry test failure, zero errors and zero
skips, in 139.588 seconds under the original 300-second cap. The failing test
assumes exactly three registry additions and a mutable historical Reflection.
Versioned historical snapshots are preserved; the test owner is coordinating
a strict version-chain repair. No current whole graph or remote CI green is
claimed, and the denied attempt remains evidence.
